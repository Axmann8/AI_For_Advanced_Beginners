# Lisbon trip (October)

## Favorites
- Pastéis de nata at Manteigaria: go early, the line gets long by 11am.
- Tram 28 is crowded; walking Alfama in the morning is nicer.
- Sunset at Miradouro da Senhora do Monte: best view in the city.

## Practical
Bring comfortable shoes: the hills and cobblestones are no joke.
The Viva Viagem card works on metro, trams, and ferries. Load it with "zapping" credit.
