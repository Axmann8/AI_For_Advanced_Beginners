# 👋 Welcome to your second brain

Everything new lands here in the **Inbox**. Don't organize while capturing: just drop things in.

Then, once a day or once a week, ask your AI helper:

> *"Triage my inbox."*

It will suggest where each note belongs (Projects, Areas, Resources or Archive), add tags and links, and wait for your OK.
