# 13 · AI Inside the Apps You Already Use 🏡

You don't always need a new tool. Many of the apps you already use have serious AI built in, and many
of them are now **MCP-connected**, so your AI can reach in and your apps can reach out.

---

## 📒 Notion: the AI-native workspace
- **Notion Agent**: your on-demand assistant. It can create and edit pages and databases, run multi-step
  tasks, and search across your workspace *and* connected apps (Slack, Drive, GitHub…).
- **Custom Agents**: team-wide AI teammates that run **on schedules or triggers** 24/7. Think
  "every Monday, compile last week's updates into a digest page." These run on Notion credits (an add-on).
- **AI Meeting Notes**: transcribes, summarizes, and pulls out action items.
- **MCP both ways**: Notion's official MCP server lets Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor read and write your
  workspace, and Notion's agents can connect to other MCP servers (Linear, Figma, HubSpot, and custom ones).
- **🎮 Try:** Build a "Second Brain" database (Inbox → Projects → Areas → Resources) and ask the
  agent to file your inbox every morning.

## 🟪 Obsidian: local-first, plugin paradise
- Your notes are plain Markdown files on your disk, which makes them perfect for AI.
- Community plugins: **Copilot for Obsidian**, **Smart Connections** (semantic "related notes"),
  **Text Generator**, and local-model support via Ollama.
- Point the **Filesystem MCP** or an Obsidian MCP server at your vault, and Claude can navigate, link, and write notes.
- **🎮 Try:** *"Read my daily notes from this month and write a reflection on recurring themes."*

## 📊 Spreadsheets & databases
- **Google Sheets + Gemini**, and the `=AI()` function for per-cell AI prompts.
- **Excel + Copilot**: analysis, formulas, charts, and Python in Excel.
- **Airtable AI**: AI fields that auto-fill, summarize, and categorize each row, plus Omni, its app-building assistant.
- **🎮 Try:** Paste 200 messy survey responses into a sheet and add AI columns for *sentiment*,
  *theme*, and *one-line summary*. Instant insight.

## ✉️ Email & calendar
- **Gmail + Gemini** and **Outlook + Copilot** handle summaries, drafting, and search.
- **Superhuman, Shortwave, and Spark** are AI-first email clients (auto-triage, AI search, "write like me").
- **Reclaim, Motion, Clockwise** do AI calendar scheduling that defends your focus time.

## 🗂️ Project & team tools
| App | AI highlights |
|---|---|
| **Linear** | AI triage and duplicate detection, agents you can assign issues to, official MCP |
| **ClickUp Brain** | Workspace Q&A, writing, auto-updates |
| **Asana AI** | AI teammates, status summaries, smart workflows |
| **Coda AI** | AI columns, packs, and doc-wide agents |
| **Slack AI** | Channel recaps, thread summaries, enterprise search, and agents in Slack |
| **Miro / FigJam AI** | Cluster sticky notes, generate diagrams, summarize boards |

## 🖥️ Desktop-level AI
- **Raycast** (Mac/Windows): hotkey-launched AI with extensions and MCP support. Very fast.
- **Claude Desktop / ChatGPT desktop**: global shortcuts, screen sharing, and local MCP.
- **Granola, Otter, Fireflies**: AI meeting notes that don't need a bot joining your call (Granola).
- **Wispr Flow, Superwhisper**: voice dictation that cleans up your rambling into polished text.

## 🎙️ Learning & reading
- **NotebookLM**: upload sources, get a grounded chat, study guides, and **AI podcast "Audio Overviews"**. (More in [Ch. 23](../part-6-knowledge-and-memory/23-rag-memory-and-knowledge.md).)
- **Readwise Reader**: AI "Ghostreader" plus highlight syncing to Notion or Obsidian.
- **Perplexity Spaces**: research workspaces with your files and the web.

---

## The "hub" strategy 🕸️
Pick **one home base** for your knowledge (Notion *or* Obsidian *or* Google Drive), then:
1. Pipe things **into** it with automations ([Ch. 9](../part-3-automation/09-automation-platforms.md)).
2. Let AI **read and write** it via MCP ([Ch. 5](../part-2-mcp-and-connectors/05-mcp-server-catalog.md)).
3. Ask questions **across** it ([Ch. 23](../part-6-knowledge-and-memory/23-rag-memory-and-knowledge.md)).

A single well-fed hub beats ten half-used apps.
