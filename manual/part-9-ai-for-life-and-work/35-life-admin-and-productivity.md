# 35 · Life Admin & Personal Productivity 🏡✅

Taxes, bills, appointments, meal plans, travel, forms, emails from the school: **life admin** eats hours
every week. AI won't do your laundry (yet 🧺), but it can take a huge bite out of the thinking, planning, and paperwork.

---

## The life-admin AI toolkit

| Task | How AI helps | 🎮 Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| 📬 **Email & inbox** | Triage, summaries, reply drafts | *"Summarize these 20 emails: what needs action, what's FYI, what's junk."* |
| 📅 **Scheduling** | Find times, plan weeks, protect focus time | *"Plan my week: here are my tasks, meetings, and energy patterns."* |
| 🍲 **Meals** | Meal plans, grocery lists, using up leftovers | *"Plan 5 dinners under 30 minutes using chicken, spinach, and rice, and make a grocery list by aisle."* |
| ✈️ **Travel** | Itineraries, packing lists, comparing options | *"3-day Kyoto itinerary for a slow traveler who loves food and gardens."* |
| 📄 **Paperwork** | Explain letters, fill forms, draft complaints | *"Explain this insurance letter in plain English and tell me what I need to do by when."* |
| 💸 **Money** | Budgets, subscription audits, comparing plans | *"Here's my bank CSV. Find subscriptions and categorize spending."* |
| 🏠 **Home** | Repairs, manuals, maintenance schedules | *"[photo] What is this part, and how do I fix the leak?"* |
| 🎁 **Relationships** | Gift ideas, remembering details, cards | *"Birthday message for my aunt who loves gardening and puns."* |
| 🩺 **Health admin** | Prep questions for appointments, understanding results | *"Help me prepare 5 questions for my doctor about [topic]."* (Your doctor still makes the calls!) |

## Systems that run themselves 🔁

### 1. The Sunday planning session (20 minutes)
Prompt with your calendar connected:
> *"Look at my calendar for next week. Here's my task list: [paste]. Build a realistic plan with focus blocks, buffer time,
> and one fun thing each day. Flag anything overloaded. Be kind about my capacity."*

### 2. The household command center (Notion or Google Sheets)
- Bills and renewal dates → automated reminders a week before each is due.
- Maintenance schedule (filters, car service, smoke alarm batteries) → recurring tasks.
- A shared family calendar → a weekly AI digest to the family group chat.

### 3. The paperwork processor
Snap photos of letters → AI extracts **deadline, action required, amount** → task created with a due date. (n8n + a vision model, or just
a quick chat.)

### 4. The subscription audit 💳
Export 3 months of transactions → *"List every recurring charge, when it renews, and which ones I might not use."*
Many people find money here on the very first try. 💰

### 5. Personal CRM for the people you love ❤️
A simple database: names, birthdays, kids' names, recent life events, gift ideas. An AI reminder a week before each
birthday with a draft message and gift suggestions.

## Productivity methods, AI-enhanced ⚡

| Method | AI twist |
|---|---|
| **Getting Things Done** | AI processes your inbox into next actions and projects |
| **Time blocking** | AI builds your blocks from tasks + calendar + energy |
| **Eat the frog** | *"Which task on this list will I most want to avoid? Break it into 10-minute steps."* |
| **Weekly review** | A skill or agent compiles your week ([example](../../examples/prompts-for-agents/skills/weekly-review/SKILL.md)) |
| **Body doubling** | Voice-mode AI as a friendly "working together" companion for focus sessions |
| **Overwhelm reset** | *"I'm overwhelmed. Here's everything on my mind: [brain dump]. Sort it into now, later, never, and give me ONE next step."* |

That last one is a real gift for busy or neurodivergent brains. 💛

## Privacy & safety for personal life 🔒
- Be thoughtful about uploading **IDs, medical records, and financial statements**. Redact account numbers, and consider local models ([Ch. 26](../part-7-local-ai/26-local-and-open-models.md)).
- AI can explain letters and contracts, but **for legal, tax, or medical decisions, confirm with a professional.**
- Keep a human check on anything that **sends money or signs up** for things.
