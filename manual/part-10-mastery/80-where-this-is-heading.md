# 80 · Where This Is All Heading 🔭✨

> ⏱️ 8 min read · 🎯 Everyone who's made it this far (you legend!) · 🧰 Needs: a cup of something warm and a bit of daydreaming time

**You've come a long way: foundations, MCP, automation, building, knowledge, local AI, creativity, life, and mastery. 🎉** Let's
zoom out. Where is all this going, and what does it mean for someone like you, who's now well ahead of most people in
understanding and *using* AI? This chapter covers eight trends that are already visible, the skills that matter most as they
unfold, a personal roadmap for your next 90 days, and a few parting thoughts before the hands-on Build-Alongs.

> [!NOTE]
> **📌 Trends, not prophecies**
> Predictions are hard, especially about the future. 😄 These are directions already visible today, and the details will
> surprise all of us.

<details class="eli5" open>
<summary>🧸 ELI5: This chapter in 30 seconds</summary>

AI is growing up fast. Soon it will do more jobs by itself, plug into almost every app, see and hear the world, remember what
matters to you, and help anyone build their own apps. The most important skills for people will be: explaining clearly what
you want, checking the work, and being kind and wise about how AI is used. You've been practicing all of those! 🌟

</details>

## 🤝 Trend 1: From chatting to delegating

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Instead of just answering questions, AI will do bigger and bigger jobs for you, like a helper who works while you do something
else and reports back.

</details>

The arc of this manual, **chat → connect → automate → build agents**, is also the arc of the industry. AI is moving from "answer my
question" to "handle this for me": longer tasks, more autonomy, background work, and reporting back.

**What it means for you:** the most valuable skill is **delegation**: clearly describing outcomes, setting up guardrails, and
reviewing work. (Sound familiar? It's managing, and you've been practicing it all manual long.)

## 🔌 Trend 2: Everything becomes connectable

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Almost every app will get a plug for AI, so AI helpers can use all your tools together.

</details>

MCP and similar standards mean every app is becoming a potential tool for AI, and the protocol keeps maturing (remote servers,
better auth, richer apps inside chats). Apps with good AI connections will win; apps without them will feel like islands.

**What it means for you:** choose tools that connect. And **building a connector** is a genuinely valuable skill
([Building MCP Servers](../part-2-mcp-and-connectors/11-building-mcp-servers.md)).

## 👀 Trend 3: AI that sees, hears and acts

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

AI will talk and listen naturally, watch videos, use computers like people do, and even help robots in the real world.

</details>

Voice agents that sound natural, computer-use agents that operate any app, models that understand video, interactive world
models, and robotics research accelerating. The interface is becoming **conversation plus delegation** instead of clicking
through menus ([Voice Agents](../part-8-creative-ai/56-voice-agents.md), [Computer Use](../part-5-building-with-ai/40-computer-use-and-browser-agents.md)).

## 🧠 Trend 4: Personal AI that knows you

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

AI helpers will remember your projects and preferences, like a friend who knows you well, and you should stay in charge of what
they remember.

</details>

Memory, connectors and personal knowledge bases are converging into assistants with rich, long-term context about your projects,
preferences and history, ideally with **you** in control of that data.

**What it means for you:** your well-organized notes, docs and systems ([Part VI](../part-6-knowledge-and-memory/index.md))
become a **compounding asset**. The better your context, the better your AI. And your privacy choices matter more than ever
([Privacy & Your Data](73-privacy-and-your-data.md)).

## 🛠️ Trend 5: Everyone can build software

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Soon anyone will be able to make their own apps just by describing them, even for tiny jobs only their family needs.

</details>

Vibe coding and coding agents are collapsing the gap between "idea" and "working tool." We're heading toward a world of
**personal software**: small, custom apps built for one person or one family, which were never worth building before.

**What it means for you:** stop waiting for someone to build the app you want. You can build it this weekend
([Vibe Coding](../part-5-building-with-ai/34-vibe-coding-your-first-app.md)).

## 💸 Trend 6: Cheaper, faster, more local

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

AI keeps getting cheaper and smaller. Things that cost a lot today will be almost free tomorrow, and will run on your own phone.

</details>

Capability keeps getting cheaper. Today's frontier becomes tomorrow's budget tier, and eventually runs on your laptop or phone
([Local & Open Models](../part-7-local-ai/47-local-and-open-models.md)).

**What it means for you:** ideas that seem too expensive today (an AI reviewing every email, photo or document) become trivially
affordable. **Design for the abundance that's coming.**

## 🌍 Trend 7: AI in science, health and learning

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

AI is starting to help scientists discover new things, help doctors, and give every student a patient tutor.

</details>

Some of the most hopeful developments are happening in science and public good: AI helping with protein and materials research,
medical imaging and drug discovery, climate and weather modeling, and personalized tutoring that can reach learners anywhere.
Progress is uneven and needs careful validation, but the potential to help people is enormous.

**What it means for you:** curiosity is rewarded. Whatever field you love, AI is changing it, and people who understand both the
field *and* the tools will lead.

## ⚖️ Trend 8: Trust, safety and judgment become the premium skills

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

As AI makes lots of stuff, being able to tell what's true, what's good and what's safe becomes super important, and that's a
human superpower.

</details>

As AI output floods the world, **judgment** grows more valuable: knowing what's true, what's good, what's safe and what matters.
So do verifying, curating, and taking responsibility. Societies are also working out the rules: transparency, safety testing,
and protections for people ([AI Ethics for Builders](76-ai-ethics-for-builders.md)).

**What it means for you:** your taste, ethics and critical thinking aren't obsolete. **They're the whole game.**

## 💛 A few parting thoughts

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

You're ahead of most people now. Stay curious, be kind, use AI to have more time for the people and things you love, and have
fun!

</details>

- **You're early.** Most people still use AI as a slightly better search box. You now know how to connect it, automate with it,
  build with it and evaluate it. That's a real advantage.
- **Stay curious, stay kind.** The best AI builders solve real problems for real people.
- **Keep your humanity at the center.** Use AI to buy back time for the things only you can do: relationships, creativity, rest,
  play.
- **Have fun.** Seriously. This is one of the most exciting times ever to be a tinkerer. 🎉

## 🎯 Key takeaways

- AI is moving from **chatting to delegating**, and everything is becoming **connectable**.
- AI increasingly **sees, hears, acts, remembers** and **builds**, while getting **cheaper and more local**.
- Some of the most hopeful progress is in **science, health and learning**.
- **Judgment, verification, ethics and clear communication** are the premium human skills.
- Pick a **90-day roadmap**, and next, get hands-on with the **Build-Alongs**.
