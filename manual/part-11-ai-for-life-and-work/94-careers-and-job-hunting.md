# 94 · Careers & Job Hunting with AI 💼🚀

> ⏱️ 7 min read · 🎯 Job seekers, career changers, students, anyone ready for a next step · 🧰 Needs: an assistant (voice mode is great for interview practice), your résumé

**Job hunting is stressful, repetitive and full of guesswork, which makes it perfect territory for an AI sidekick.** AI can
help you figure out what you actually want, tailor every application honestly, practice interviews out loud until you're
confident, negotiate better offers, and make a great start in your new role. This chapter is your complete career toolkit,
plus the honesty rules that keep you trusted. You've got this! 💪

<details class="eli5" open>
<summary>🧸 ELI5: This chapter in 30 seconds</summary>

Finding a job is like a big quest. AI can be your quest guide: it helps you figure out which jobs would make you happy,
makes your résumé shine for each job (without fibbing!), pretends to be the interviewer so you can practice, and helps you
ask for a fair salary. You still do the talking and the deciding, but you'll feel way more ready. 🗺️✨

</details>

## 🧭 Figure out what you want

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Before hunting, figure out what makes you happy at work: what you're good at, what you enjoy, and what matters to you. AI can
ask you great questions to help.

</details>

| Goal | Prompt |
|---|---|
| **Self-discovery** | *"Interview me like a career coach, one question at a time, about what energizes and drains me at work. Then summarize patterns."* |
| **Explore paths** | *"Based on my skills [list] and interests [list], suggest 10 roles, including 3 I've probably never heard of."* |
| **Reality check** | *"What does a typical week look like for a [role]? What do people love and hate about it?"* |
| **Skill gaps** | *"Compare my experience with typical requirements for [role]. What are my 3 biggest gaps, and the fastest ways to close them?"* |
| **Career change** | *"I'm a teacher moving into instructional design. Which of my skills transfer, and how should I describe them?"* |

> [!TIP]
> **💡 Talk to real humans too**
> AI gives you a map; people give you the real territory. Ask AI to draft **5 questions** for informational chats with people in
> roles you're curious about, then actually go have those coffees. ☕

## 📄 Résumés that get read

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

A résumé is a one-page story of what you've done. AI helps make it clear, tailored to each job, and full of real results, but
never with made-up stuff.

</details>

**Step 1 · Build a "master résumé":** everything you've ever done, with numbers. Ask AI to interview you to dig out
accomplishments:

> *"Ask me questions about each of my past jobs to uncover measurable achievements. Push me for numbers (time saved, money,
> people, percentages)."*

**Step 2 · Tailor for each job:**

> *"Here's my master résumé and this job description. Choose the most relevant experience, rewrite bullets to match the
> job's language where it's truthful, and quantify impact. Don't invent anything. Flag gaps I should address in the cover letter."*

| Weak bullet | Strong bullet |
|---|---|
| "Responsible for social media." | "Grew Instagram from 2k to 11k followers in 9 months with a weekly Reels series." |
| "Helped customers." | "Resolved ~40 support tickets a day with a 96% satisfaction score." |
| "Worked on the website." | "Rebuilt the booking page, cutting drop-offs by 30%." |

**Many employers use applicant tracking systems (ATS):** keep formatting simple (no text in images, standard headings), and
include the job's key skills *if you truly have them*.

> [!WARNING]
> **⚠️ Never invent experience**
> Fabricated skills, titles or degrees get discovered, and they can cost you the job later. AI should make the **truth** shine,
> not create fiction. Always review every line before sending.

## ✉️ Cover letters & outreach

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

A cover letter says "here's why I'd be great for this job and why I want it." AI helps you write it, but your real reasons
make it special.

</details>

- **Cover letter:** *"Write a 250-word cover letter for this job. Open with this specific story: [your story]. Connect my
  experience to their 3 biggest needs. Sound like me: [style notes]."*
- **Why this company:** research first (their blog, news, products), then add one genuine, specific reason you're excited.
- **Networking messages:** *"Draft a short LinkedIn message to a [role] at [company]. Mention [specific thing they wrote]. Ask
  for 15 minutes of advice, not a job."*
- **Follow-ups:** *"A polite follow-up email one week after my interview, mentioning [topic we discussed]."*

## 🔎 Smarter job searching

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Instead of scrolling job sites for hours, let AI help find good matches, track your applications, and remind you to follow up.

</details>

| Task | AI approach |
|---|---|
| **Find roles** | Job sites' AI search + saved alerts. Or a custom alert: [Web Scraping & Monitoring](../part-5-automation/51-web-scraping-and-monitoring.md) |
| **Screen postings** | *"Score this job against my must-haves [list] from 1–10, and list red flags."* |
| **Track applications** | A spreadsheet or Notion board: company, role, date, status, next step, contact |
| **Company research** | Deep research: *"Brief me on [company]: products, recent news, culture signals, likely interview topics."* |
| **Keep momentum** | A weekly review: *"Here's my tracker. What should I follow up on, and what's working?"* |

**Build it:** a job-tracker app is a perfect first vibe-coding project ([Vibe Coding](../part-7-building-with-ai/65-vibe-coding-your-first-app.md)).

## 🎤 Interview practice (the superpower)

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Practice interviews out loud with AI pretending to be the interviewer. It asks tough questions, listens, and gives you tips,
as many times as you want, with zero embarrassment.

</details>

Use **voice mode** so you practice speaking, not typing ([Voice Agents](../part-10-creative-ai/87-voice-agents.md)):

```text
You're interviewing me for [role] at [company]. Here's the job description: [paste].
Ask one question at a time, mixing behavioral, technical and "tell me about yourself" questions.
After each answer, give brief feedback: what was strong, what to tighten, and a better structure if needed.
Be realistic but kind. After 8 questions, give me an overall score and my top 3 improvements.
```

**Build a story bank with STAR** (Situation, Task, Action, Result):

> *"Help me turn these 6 experiences into STAR stories under 2 minutes each. Which questions could each story answer?"*

| Practice mode | Prompt |
|---|---|
| **Curveballs** | *"Ask me the 5 hardest questions you'd expect for this role."* |
| **Weakness question** | *"Help me answer 'What's your biggest weakness?' honestly and constructively."* |
| **Career gap** | *"Help me explain my 2-year gap caring for a parent, briefly and confidently."* |
| **Technical** | *"Give me 3 practice problems at interview level for [skill], then review my answers."* |
| **My questions** | *"Suggest 8 smart questions to ask the interviewer about this team."* |

## 💰 Negotiating offers

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

When you get a job offer, it's normal and OK to ask for more. AI helps you find out what's fair and practice what to say.

</details>

1. **Research the range:** salary sites, public pay bands, and deep research for your role, level and location.
2. **Know your value:** *"Summarize my strongest leverage points for this offer."*
3. **Script it:** *"Write a warm, confident email asking for a higher base salary of [X], citing [reasons]."*
4. **Rehearse live:** *"Play the hiring manager. Push back realistically. Help me stay calm and firm."*
5. **Think total compensation:** base, bonus, equity, benefits, flexibility, learning budget, title, start date.

## 🏁 Your first 90 days

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Once you get the job, AI helps you learn fast, plan your first months, and remember who everyone is and what they need.

</details>

- **30-60-90 plan:** *"Draft a 30-60-90 day plan for my new role as [role], based on these notes about the team's goals."*
- **Learn the domain:** a tutor persona for your new industry ([Research & Learning](91-research-and-learning.md)).
- **Meeting notes:** AI notes → your own decisions log ([Email & Calendar](../part-6-ai-in-your-apps/57-email-and-calendar.md)).
- **Check your company's AI policy first**, and never paste confidential data into tools that aren't approved.

## ⚖️ Honesty rules

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Use AI to prepare, but be yourself in the actual interview, and never lie about what you've done.

</details>

| Do ✅ | Don't ❌ |
|---|---|
| Use AI to prepare, practice and polish | Invent experience, degrees or skills |
| Review every line before sending | Send AI text you haven't read |
| Follow each employer's rules on AI use | Secretly use AI during live interviews or tests unless allowed |
| Make your applications specific and personal | Mass-send identical AI-generated applications |
| Protect your personal data | Paste confidential employer data into public tools |

## 🎯 Key takeaways

- Use AI to **discover** what you want, then talk to real people in those roles.
- Build a **master résumé**, then **tailor honestly** for each job with quantified results.
- **Practice interviews in voice mode** with a STAR story bank, until you feel calm.
- **Negotiate** with research, a script and rehearsal. Think total compensation.
- Your **AI skills** are a career asset, and **honesty** keeps your reputation strong.

<details class="quiz">

**No.** Never invent experience. Address the gap honestly in your cover letter or show how you're learning it.

</details>

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 2. What's the best way to practice interviews with AI?</summary>

**Voice mode** mock interviews with feedback after each answer, using a **STAR story bank**.

</details>

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 3. What's a "master résumé"?</summary>

A complete record of **everything** you've done with measurable results, from which you **tailor** each application.

</details>
