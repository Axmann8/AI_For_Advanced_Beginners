# 100 · Home, Cooking & DIY with AI 🏠🍳🔧

> ⏱️ 7 min read · 🎯 Home cooks, fixers, gardeners, decorators, renters and homeowners · 🧰 Needs: an assistant with vision (photos!), optionally a smart home setup

**Your phone's camera + an AI assistant = a chef, a handyperson, a gardener and an interior designer in your pocket.** Snap your
fridge and get dinner ideas. Snap a leaky pipe and learn what that part is called. Snap a sad plant and find out why it's sad.
This chapter covers cooking, DIY repairs (and when to call a pro), home maintenance, gardening, decorating, cleaning, moving,
pets and the smart home. Let's make home life easier and more fun. 🌿🏡

<details class="eli5" open>
<summary>🧸 ELI5: This chapter in 30 seconds</summary>

Take a photo of anything in your house and ask AI about it: "what can I cook with this?", "what is this broken thing?", "why
are my plant's leaves yellow?" It explains, gives you step-by-step instructions, and warns you when a job is dangerous and you
should call a grown-up expert, like an electrician. 📸🔧

</details>

## 🍳 Cooking: your pocket chef

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Show AI what's in your fridge and it suggests dinners. It can also change recipes, swap ingredients, and teach you cooking
tricks.

</details>

| Situation | Prompt |
|---|---|
| 📸 **What's for dinner?** | Photo of fridge + pantry → *"3 dinners I can make tonight in 30 minutes. Nothing too spicy."* |
| 🔁 **Substitutions** | *"I'm out of buttermilk. What can I use in pancakes?"* |
| ⚖️ **Scaling** | *"Scale this recipe from 4 to 10 servings and convert to grams."* |
| 🌱 **Dietary changes** | *"Make this lasagna vegetarian and dairy-free without losing the comfort."* |
| 🧑‍🍳 **Technique coach** | *"How do I know when onions are properly caramelized? What goes wrong?"* |
| 🍞 **Baking science** | *"Why did my bread come out dense? Here's what I did: [steps]."* |
| 🥡 **Leftovers** | *"Turn leftover roast chicken, rice and half a cabbage into something exciting."* |
| 🗓️ **Meal prep** | *"A Sunday batch-cook plan for 5 lunches, with an order of operations."* |
| 🛒 **Grocery list** | *"Grocery list for these 5 dinners, grouped by aisle, minus what I already have."* |

> [!WARNING]
> **⚠️ Food safety & allergies**
> Always state allergies clearly, double-check ingredient labels yourself, and follow official food-safety guidance for
> cooking temperatures, storage and canning. AI can make mistakes, and food safety isn't the place to guess.

**Family recipe rescue:** photograph grandma's handwritten recipe cards → *"Transcribe these, standardize the measurements, and
fill in missing steps (mark them as guesses)."* Then make a family cookbook ([Image Generation](../part-10-creative-ai/84-image-generation-deep-dive.md)). 👵📖

## 🔧 DIY & repairs

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Show AI a photo of something broken, and it tells you what the part is called, how to fix it step by step, and which tools you
need, or tells you to call an expert if it's dangerous.

</details>

**The DIY diagnosis prompt:**

> *[photo]* *"What is this part, what's likely wrong, and how would I fix it step by step? List tools and parts needed, how
> hard it is (1–5), and any safety risks. Tell me honestly if I should call a professional."*

| Great DIY uses | Example |
|---|---|
| **Identify parts** | "It's a compression fitting on a shutoff valve." Now you can buy the right one |
| **Step-by-step guides** | Patching drywall, fixing a running toilet, re-caulking a bath |
| **Tool advice** | *"Which drill bit for tiles?"* |
| **Estimate materials** | *"How much paint for a 4 × 5 m room with 2.6 m ceilings, 2 coats?"* |
| **Read manuals** | Upload the appliance manual PDF → *"What does error E24 mean?"* |
| **Compare quotes** | *"Compare these 3 contractor quotes. What's missing or suspicious?"* |

> [!CAUTION]
> **🔐 Call a licensed professional for dangerous jobs**
> **Electrical wiring, gas, structural changes, roofing, asbestos, and anything that needs permits** should be done by
> licensed professionals. AI can help you understand the problem and talk to the pro, but a mistake here can hurt people.

## 🗓️ Home maintenance on autopilot

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Houses need regular care, like changing filters and testing alarms. AI makes a schedule, and robots remind you when it's time.

</details>

> *"Create a seasonal home maintenance schedule for a 1990s house in a cold, snowy climate with a gas furnace, a lawn and gutters.
> Group by month, with how long each task takes."*

Put it in your household command center with automatic reminders ([Life Admin](95-life-admin-and-productivity.md#-system-2-the-household-command-center)).

| Season | Typical tasks |
|---|---|
| 🌸 **Spring** | Clean gutters, check the roof, service the AC |
| ☀️ **Summer** | Check decks and outdoor wood, clean dryer vents |
| 🍂 **Autumn** | Service the heating, clear gutters, check weatherstripping |
| ❄️ **Winter** | Test smoke and CO alarms, protect pipes, check for drafts |

## 🌱 Gardening & plants

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Take a photo of a plant and AI tells you what it is, why it looks sick, and when to plant things in your garden.

</details>

| Need | Prompt |
|---|---|
| 🔎 **Identify** | *[photo]* *"What plant is this, and is it safe for cats?"* 🐱 |
| 🩺 **Diagnose** | *[photo]* *"Why are these tomato leaves yellow with brown spots? Most likely causes first."* |
| 📅 **Planting calendar** | *"What can I plant in [region/zone] this month? A beginner-friendly list."* |
| 🗺️ **Garden plan** | *"Design a small raised-bed vegetable garden for a sunny 3 × 1 m spot, with companion planting."* |
| 🪴 **Houseplants** | *"Low-light, pet-safe houseplants that are hard to kill."* |
| 🐛 **Pests** | *"Gentle, garden-friendly ways to deal with aphids."* |

**Garden diary:** voice notes on what you planted, when, and how it went → next year's AI-powered plan ([Memory for Agents](../part-8-knowledge-and-memory/75-memory-for-agents.md)).

## 🛋️ Decorating & organizing

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Show AI a photo of your room and it can show you what it might look like painted a new color or with different furniture,
and help you tidy up.

</details>

- **Visualize changes:** photo of your room → *"Show this room with sage-green walls and warmer lighting."* ([Image Generation](../part-10-creative-ai/84-image-generation-deep-dive.md#-editing-the-real-superpower))
- **Style finder:** *"Here are 5 rooms I love. What's my style called, and what are its key ingredients?"*
- **Small spaces:** *"Layout ideas for a 3 × 4 m bedroom that needs a desk."*
- **Budget makeover:** *"5 changes under $100 that would make this living room feel cozier."*
- **Declutter plan:** *"A 30-day decluttering challenge, 15 minutes a day, room by room."*
- **Cleaning routine:** *"A weekly cleaning schedule for a 2-bedroom apartment, split between 2 people fairly."*
- **Stain SOS:** *"Red wine on a wool rug. What do I do right now?"* (test on a hidden spot first)

## 📦 Moving house

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Moving has a million little jobs. AI makes a checklist, a packing plan, and reminds you to tell everyone your new address.

</details>

> *"Create an 8-week moving checklist: from [city] to [city], 2 adults, a cat, a car. Include address-change list, utilities,
> packing order and a first-night box."*

Add: a room-by-room box inventory, a comparison of moving company quotes, and an **"update my address"** checklist (bank,
doctor, subscriptions, voter registration, deliveries).

## 🐾 Pets

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

AI can help you care for your pet: training tips, feeding schedules and understanding their behavior. For anything health-related,
you still call the vet.

</details>

- **Training:** *"A gentle, positive-reinforcement plan to stop my puppy jumping on guests."*
- **Care routines:** feeding, grooming and exercise schedules for a new pet.
- **Behavior:** *"My cat started scratching the sofa. Likely reasons and humane fixes."*
- **Safety:** *"Which common plants and foods are toxic to dogs?"*
- **Vet prep:** a symptom summary and questions for the vet. **For health concerns, always contact your vet.** 🐶

## 🏡 The AI-powered smart home

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

A smart home has lights, thermostats and speakers you can control with your voice or phone. AI can make it smarter, like
turning off lights when everyone leaves.

</details>

| Level | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| 🟢 **Voice assistants** | Newer AI-powered versions of Alexa, Google and Siri control lights and answer questions |
| 🟡 **Routines** | "Good night" turns off lights, locks doors and sets the thermostat |
| 🔴 **Home Assistant + AI** | A private, powerful hub that works with thousands of devices, a local LLM and an MCP server ([Build-Along: Private Home Assistant](../part-13-build-alongs/117-build-along-private-home-assistant.md)) |

**Fun automations:** lights that wake you gently, a "laundry's done" notification, a daily "did anyone water the plants?"
reminder, a morning briefing on the kitchen speaker. 🌅

## 🎯 Key takeaways

- **Photos + AI** = instant help identifying, diagnosing and deciding at home.
- In the kitchen: **fridge-to-dinner, substitutions, scaling and technique coaching**, with care for allergies and food safety.
- For DIY: **diagnose, plan, and estimate materials**, and call **licensed pros** for electrical, gas and structural work.
- Automate **maintenance**, **garden diaries** and **moving checklists**.
- The smart home ranges from **voice assistants to private Home Assistant setups**.

## 🧠 Check yourself

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 1. A light switch sparks when you flip it. Should you follow AI's DIY instructions?</summary>

**No.** Stop using it and call a **licensed electrician**. AI can help you describe the problem, but electrical work is for
pros.

</details>

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 2. What's the best way to get dinner ideas from what you have?</summary>

**Photograph your fridge and pantry** and ask for quick recipes with your constraints (time, diet, spice level).

</details>

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 3. Your dog ate something odd and seems unwell. What's the first step?</summary>

**Contact your vet** (or an emergency vet or pet poison line) right away.

</details>
