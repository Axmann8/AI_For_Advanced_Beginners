# 28 · The Multimodal Playground: Images, Video, Voice, Music & Computer Use 🎨🎬🎵

Text is just the beginning. This is the chapter where you make things you can *see and hear*.

## 🖼️ Images
| Tool | Superpower |
|---|---|
| **Midjourney** | Gorgeous, artistic aesthetics. Also does video now |

**🎮 Try:** Design a full brand kit for an imaginary business (logo, palette, social post, and a mockup of it on a mug) using 2–3 of these tools.

## 🖱️ Computer use & browser agents: AI that uses your apps
The AI **looks at the screen and clicks, types, and scrolls** like a person.
| Tool | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Claude computer use / Claude in Chrome** | Claude operates a browser or desktop to finish tasks |
| **ChatGPT agent** | A browser plus terminal inside ChatGPT for multi-step web tasks |
| **Gemini in Chrome / Project Mariner** | Google's browser agent |
| **Perplexity Comet, OpenAI Atlas, other AI browsers** | Browsers with an agent built in |
| **Playwright MCP / Browserbase / Browser Use** | Build-your-own browser automation for agents |

**Good uses:** filling tedious forms, comparing prices across sites, and gathering data from sites
with no API. **Be careful:** keep payments and logins supervised, and watch for pages that try to
hijack the agent (see [Ch. 37](../part-10-mastery/37-safety-costs-and-gotchas.md)).

## 🧪 Multimodal *inputs* (underrated!)
You can **give** AI images, screenshots, PDFs, audio, and video, not just get them back:
- Screenshot a confusing error, chart, or settings page and ask "what am I looking at?"
- Photograph your fridge: *"Dinner ideas with what's here?"*
- Upload a whiteboard photo: *"Turn this into a Mermaid diagram and a task list."*
- Feed Gemini a YouTube link or long video: *"At what timestamp do they explain X?"*
