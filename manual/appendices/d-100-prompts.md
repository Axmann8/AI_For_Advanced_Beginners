# Appendix D · 100 Prompts That Use Tools 💬🔧

These aren't "write me a poem" prompts. Every one of them **uses tools**: connectors, MCP servers, web search, code execution,
or automations. Each is tagged with what it needs. Copy, tweak, and go!

**Tags:** 📧 email · 📅 calendar · 📁 files/Drive · 📒 Notion · 💬 Slack · 🐙 GitHub · 🔎 web search · 🌐 fetch/browser ·
🐍 code execution · 🧠 memory · ⚙️ automation · 🏠 smart home · 🎨 creative tools

---

## ☀️ Daily life & productivity (1–20)
1. 📧📅 *"Give me a morning briefing: today's meetings with prep notes, emails needing a reply, and my top 3 priorities."*
2. 📧 *"Find every email where someone asked me a question I haven't answered this week."*
3. 📅 *"Find three 1-hour focus blocks next week and add them to my calendar as 'Deep work'."*
4. 📧📅 *"Someone emailed asking to meet. Propose 3 times that fit my calendar and draft the reply."*
5. 📧 *"Unsubscribe candidates: list newsletters I haven't opened in a month."*
6. 📁 *"What have I been working on most this month, based on my recently edited files?"*
7. 📒 *"Turn my brain dump into tasks in my Notion Tasks database with due dates and priorities."*
8. 📅 *"How many hours of meetings did I have this month vs. last month? Which recurring ones could I drop?"*
9. 🧠 *"Remember that I prefer morning meetings, hate Mondays before 10, and my partner's name is Jordan."*
10. 📧 *"Draft a polite 'no' to this request that keeps the relationship warm."*
11. 🔎📅 *"Is it going to rain during my 5pm run tomorrow? Suggest a better time if so."*
12. 📁 *"Find the lease agreement in my Drive and tell me the notice period for moving out."*
13. 📒 *"Create a packing list page for my Lisbon trip based on the weather forecast."*
14. 📧 *"Summarize this 40-message thread: decisions made, open questions, and who owes what."*
15. 📅📒 *"After each meeting today, create a Notion note with attendees pre-filled."*
16. ⚙️ *"Run my 'weekly review' workflow and show me the result."*
17. 📁🐍 *"Here's my bank CSV. Categorize spending, find subscriptions, and chart the trends."*
18. 🔎 *"Compare the 3 best-reviewed robot vacuums under $400 right now, in a table."*
19. 📧 *"Draft a friendly follow-up to everyone I emailed last week who hasn't replied."*
20. 🧠 *"What do you remember about my goals this quarter? Help me check in on progress."*
