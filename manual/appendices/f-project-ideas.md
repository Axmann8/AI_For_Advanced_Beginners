# Appendix F · 80 Project Ideas to Level Up 🚀

> ⏱️ 12 min read · 🎯 Everyone who learns by building · 🧰 Needs: curiosity and a free afternoon

**Reading is great, but building is where it sticks.** Here are 80 projects, from 🟢 weekend-afternoon to 🔴 ambitious, plus
ideas sorted by passion. Every one teaches something real, and most link to the chapter or build-along that shows you how.

> [!TIP]
> **💡 How to pick**
> Choose something that fixes an **actual annoyance in your life** or makes someone you love smile. You'll finish it.

<details class="eli5" open>
<summary>🧸 ELI5: This page in 30 seconds</summary>

This is a big list of fun things to build with AI, from super easy to super challenging. Pick one that sounds exciting, and
the list tells you which tools to use and what you'll learn. 🧱✨

</details>
