## 🖨️ Printing tips

| If you want to… | Try this |
|---|---|
| **Print just one part** | Look up the part's page range in the Contents (page 4 onwards), then print only those pages |
| **Save paper** | Print double-sided, two pages per sheet. The text is sized to stay readable |
| **Save ink** | Choose "grayscale" in the print dialog. The boxes and diagrams still work in black and white |
| **Make a real book** | Most copy shops can print and **spiral-bind** it for you. Great as a gift for someone starting out! 🎁 |
| **Read on a tablet** | Use the **A4** edition, and tap the bookmarks panel to jump anywhere |
| **Print for a beginner** | Part I · *AI from Zero* plus the cheat sheet and checklists make a lovely starter booklet |
