# 09 · Talk, Show & Snap: Voice, Photos, Files & Pictures 🎙️

> ⏱️ 15 min read + play time · 🎯 Complete beginners · 🧰 Needs: an assistant app on your phone (for voice and camera)

**Typing is only the beginning.** Today's assistants can **talk** with you out loud, **see** through your camera,
**read** your documents and **create** pictures from a sentence. For many people, this is where AI goes from "neat" to
"how did I live without this?" This chapter shows you how to use each superpower in the popular apps, with fun first
things to try.

<details class="eli5" open>
<summary>🧸 ELI5: This chapter in 30 seconds</summary>

Your AI can do more than text. You can **talk** to it like a phone call, **show** it things with your camera ("what
plant is this?"), **give** it documents to read ("what does this letter mean?"), and ask it to **draw** pictures ("a
cat astronaut!"). It's like your helper got ears, eyes and paintbrushes.

</details>

## 🎙️ Talk to it: voice mode

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Tap the voice button and just talk, like a phone call. The AI talks back, and you can interrupt it any time.

</details>

**Voice mode** turns your assistant into someone you can chat with out loud: on a walk, while cooking, in the car
(hands-free!), or when typing is a pain. Modern voice modes are genuinely conversational: you can interrupt, change your
mind mid-sentence, and it responds in a natural voice with feeling.

=== "💬 ChatGPT"

    Tap the **voice / waveform icon** next to the message box. Talk naturally; tap to interrupt. Pick a voice in
    **Settings → Voice**. On phones you can also share your **camera** or **screen** during a voice chat.

=== "✨ Gemini"

    Tap **Live** (the waveform icon) to start **Gemini Live**. On Android, hold the power button or say "Hey Google"
    first. You can turn on the **camera** or **share your screen** and ask about what it sees.

=== "🧡 Claude"

    Tap the **voice icon** in the Claude app to start a spoken conversation. Choose a voice in settings. It's great for
    thinking out loud and talking through ideas.

=== "🪟 Copilot"

    Tap the **microphone** in the Copilot app, or press the Copilot key / say "Hey Copilot" on Windows (if enabled).
    Great for quick questions while you work.

=== "⚡ Grok · 👓 Meta AI"

    - **Grok:** tap the voice icon in the Grok app; it offers several personalities and voices.
    - **Meta AI:** tap the voice icon in the Meta AI app or WhatsApp's Meta AI chat, or just talk to your **Ray-Ban Meta**
      glasses ("Hey Meta…").

**Great first things to say:**

- 🚶 *"I'm going for a 20-minute walk. Tell me something fascinating about octopuses, and let me ask questions."*
- 🍳 *"Walk me through making pancakes step by step. Wait for me to say 'next' before each step."*
- 🗣️ *"Let's practice Spanish. Speak slowly, keep it simple, and correct me gently."*
- 🧠 *"I need to think through a decision about [changing jobs]. Ask me questions and help me weigh it up."*
- 🎤 *"I have a presentation tomorrow. Let me practice it on you, then give me three tips."*

> [!TIP]
> **💡 Voice is brilliant for accessibility**
> If typing is hard (arthritis, dyslexia, low vision, or just a tiny phone keyboard), voice mode can make AI easier to
> use than anything else on your phone. See [Accessibility & AI](../part-11-ai-for-life-and-work/101-accessibility-and-ai.md).

## 📸 Show it things: photos and the live camera

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Take a photo of something and ask about it: a plant, a menu in another language, a broken thing, a confusing sign. The
AI looks and explains.

</details>

Tap the **📷 camera** or **➕** button, take or choose a photo, and ask a question about it. This is one of the most
useful things AI does:

| Snap a photo of… | And ask… |
|---|---|
| 🌿 A plant or mushroom | *"What plant is this and how do I keep it alive?"* (never eat a foraged mushroom based on AI!) |
| 🍽️ A menu in another language | *"Translate this menu and recommend a vegetarian dish."* |
| 🧾 A confusing sign, form or letter | *"What does this mean and what do I need to do?"* |
| 🔧 A broken thing | *"My dishwasher shows this light. What's wrong and what can I check?"* |
| 🥫 Your fridge or pantry | *"What can I cook with what you see?"* |
| 🧮 Homework | *"Don't give me the answer. Explain how to solve question 3 step by step."* |
| 👗 An outfit | *"Does this work for a summer wedding? Suggest shoes."* |
| 📊 A chart or screenshot | *"Explain what this graph shows in simple words."* |
| 🏛️ A building or artwork on holiday | *"What is this and what's the story behind it?"* |

**Live camera:** In **Gemini Live** and **ChatGPT's voice mode** (and on Ray-Ban Meta glasses), you can point your
camera and talk about what it sees *in real time*: *"I'm looking at the back of my TV. Which cable goes where?"* It feels
like video-calling a patient expert. 📹

## 📄 Give it documents to read

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

You can give the AI a whole document, like a long letter or a manual, and ask it questions about it, so you don't have
to read every word yourself.

</details>

Tap **📎 / ➕ → Upload file** (or drag a file into the chat on a computer). Assistants can read:

- 📕 **PDFs:** contracts, manuals, school letters, reports, recipes
- 📝 **Documents:** Word, Google Docs, text files
- 📊 **Spreadsheets:** Excel and CSV files (great for "what does this data show?")
- 🖼️ **Images and screenshots** of text

**Brilliant document prompts:**

- *"Summarize this in 5 bullet points, then list anything I need to **do** and any **deadlines**."*
- *"What's the cancellation policy in this contract? Quote the exact section."*
- *"Explain this [insurance policy] in plain English. What's covered and what isn't?"*
- *"I'm a [tenant]. Is there anything in this [lease] I should be worried about?"*
- *"Turn this [30-page report] into a one-page summary for my [manager]."*
- *"Answer **only** from this document. If it's not in there, say you don't know."*

> [!WARNING]
> **⚠️ Think before you upload**
> Don't upload documents full of other people's private information, or confidential work files, unless your
> assistant and workplace allow it. Blank out ID numbers and account details on scans. See
> [Staying Safe](11-staying-safe-with-ai.md).

## 🖼️ Make pictures from words

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Describe a picture in words and the AI paints it for you in seconds. You can also give it a photo and ask it to change
things.

</details>

Many assistants can **create images** from a description. Just ask *"Create an image of…"* or *"Draw…"*:

| Assistant | How to make images |
|---|---|
| 💬 **ChatGPT** | Just ask in chat; edit by describing changes ("make the sky pink") |
| ✨ **Gemini** | Ask in chat (Google's "Nano Banana" image models); excellent at editing your own photos |
| ⚡ **Grok** | **Grok Imagine**: images and short videos |
| 🪟 **Copilot** | Ask in chat for image creation |
| 👓 **Meta AI** | Type "Imagine…" in Meta AI or WhatsApp |
| 🧡 **Claude** | Doesn't make photos, but can create diagrams, charts and simple graphics |

**Tips for great images:** describe the **subject**, **style**, **setting**, **mood** and **format**:

> *"A cozy watercolor illustration of a ginger cat reading a book by a rainy window, warm lamp light, soft colors,
> square format."*

**Fun first tries:**

- 🎂 A custom birthday card image with the person's hobbies
- 🐶 Your pet as a superhero (upload a photo and ask!)
- 🏡 "What would my living room look like painted sage green?" (upload a photo)
- 📚 An illustration for a bedtime story you wrote together

> [!CAUTION]
> **🛑 Be kind with real people's images**
> Never create images of real people in embarrassing, sexual or misleading situations, and don't pass AI images off as
> real photos. It can hurt people and, in many places, it's illegal. More in [Image Generation
> Deep Dive](../part-10-creative-ai/84-image-generation-deep-dive.md).

## 🎬 Bonus: video, music and more

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Some AIs can even make short videos and songs from a description. It's amazing fun to try.

</details>

Once you're comfortable, there's a whole creative playground:

- 🎬 **Short videos from a description:** Gemini (Google's Veo models), Grok Imagine, Meta AI and others
- 🎵 **Songs:** apps like Suno create full songs from a few words ("a country song about my cat's hatred of Mondays")
- 🎧 **Podcasts from your documents:** Gemini Notebook's (formerly NotebookLM) "Audio Overview" turns notes into a
  two-host radio show
- 🗣️ **Narration:** have text read aloud in natural voices

## 🔒 Voice and photo privacy in a nutshell

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Photos and voice recordings can show more than you think, like your address on an envelope. Take a quick look before
you share.

</details>

- **Check the background** of photos: addresses on envelopes, family photos, screens, documents on the desk.
- **Voice recordings** may be stored with your chats. Check your assistant's settings to review or delete them.
- **Other people:** ask before photographing people for AI, and never upload photos of other people's children.
- **Screen sharing:** close private tabs and messages before sharing your screen.

## 🎯 Key takeaways

- **Voice mode** makes AI a conversation partner: walks, cooking, language practice, thinking out loud.
- **Photos and the live camera** let AI see: plants, menus, broken things, forms, homework, charts.
- **Uploading documents** turns long, confusing paperwork into clear summaries and answers. Say "answer only from
  this document" for accuracy.
- **Image creation** turns a sentence into a picture: describe subject, style, setting, mood.
- **Check before you share**: backgrounds, other people and sensitive documents.

## 🧠 Check yourself

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 1. You're on holiday and can't read the menu. What's the fastest help?</summary>

Snap a **photo of the menu** in your assistant and ask it to translate and recommend dishes.

</details>

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 2. How do you get accurate answers about a 40-page contract?</summary>

**Upload it** and ask specific questions, telling the AI to **answer only from the document** and quote the relevant
section.

</details>

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 3. What should you check before sending the AI a photo of your kitchen table?</summary>

The **background**: letters with your address, bank cards, documents, family photos, screens.

</details>

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 4. Your child asks the AI for help with a math worksheet photo. What's a good way to ask?</summary>

*"Don't give me the answers. Explain how to solve question 3 step by step."* It turns AI into a tutor instead of a
cheat sheet.

</details>

> painting style you love. Three superpowers unlocked. 🦸
