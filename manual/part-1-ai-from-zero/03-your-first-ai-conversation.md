# 03 · Your First AI Conversation, Step by Step 👋

> ⏱️ 20 min hands-on · 🎯 Complete beginners · 🧰 Needs: a phone or computer with internet, an email address

**Today's the day you actually talk to an AI.** This chapter holds your hand from "where do I even go?" to a real,
useful conversation: getting in, finding your way around the screen, ten great first messages, the buttons that matter,
and what to do when something looks odd. It works with ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot and friends, so pick whichever
you like. 🎉

<details class="eli5" open>
<summary>🧸 ELI5: This chapter in 30 seconds</summary>

Go to the AI's website or app, sign in like you would for email, and type a question into the box at the bottom, just
like texting. The AI texts back. You can ask follow-up questions, ask it to change its answer, or start a brand-new chat
any time. That's honestly all there is to it, and this chapter shows you each click.

</details>

## 🚪 Step 1: Get in the door

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Pick one AI, open its website or download its app, and sign in. Some let you try without an account at all.

</details>

Pick **one** assistant for today. Can't decide? Choose **ChatGPT** or **Gemini** (the most common) or **Claude**
(famously friendly and clear). You can try the others later, and [Choosing Your First AI
Assistant](04-choosing-your-first-assistant.md) helps you choose for the long run.

=== "💬 ChatGPT"

    1. Go to **chatgpt.com** in any web browser, or install the **ChatGPT** app (App Store / Google Play; look for
       **OpenAI** as the maker).
    2. You can chat right away without an account, but **Sign up** (free) to save your chats and unlock more features.
       Use your email, or "Continue with Google / Apple / Microsoft."
    3. Confirm your email and enter your name and birthday when asked.

=== "✨ Gemini"

    1. Go to **gemini.google.com**, or on Android open the **Gemini** app (often already installed; long-press the
       power button or say "Hey Google"). On iPhone, install **Google Gemini** from the App Store.
    2. Sign in with your **Google account** (the same one as Gmail or YouTube).
    3. Accept the terms. That's it!

=== "🧡 Claude"

    1. Go to **claude.ai**, or install the **Claude** app (maker: **Anthropic**) on phone or computer.
    2. Sign up with your email (it sends a login link, no password needed) or "Continue with Google."
    3. Enter your name and confirm your age.

=== "🪟 Copilot"

    1. Go to **copilot.microsoft.com**, open **Copilot** in Windows (the Copilot key or the taskbar icon), or install
       the **Microsoft Copilot** app.
    2. You can try it signed out, but signing in with a **Microsoft account** (Outlook, Xbox or Office login) saves
       your chats.

=== "👓 Meta AI & others"

    - **Meta AI** is already inside **WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger**: look for the colorful circle icon or search
      "Meta AI" in your chats. There's also a standalone **Meta AI** app.
    - **Perplexity** (perplexity.ai), **Grok** (grok.com or inside X), **DeepSeek** (chat.deepseek.com) and **Le Chat**
      (chat.mistral.ai) work the same way: open, sign in, type.

> [!WARNING]
> **⚠️ Only download official apps**
> Fake "AI" apps with names like "Chat AI GPT Pro Assistant" are everywhere, and some charge sneaky subscriptions. Check
> the **developer name** (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Microsoft, Meta, xAI, Perplexity AI…) before installing. More in
> [Staying Safe](11-staying-safe-with-ai.md).

## 🖥️ Step 2: A quick tour of the screen

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Every AI app looks a lot like a texting app: a box at the bottom for your message, the conversation in the middle, and a
list of old chats on the side.

</details>

Every chatbot has the same basic layout, whatever the brand:

| Where | What it is | What you do with it |
|---|---|---|
| ⬇️ **Bottom:** the message box | "Ask anything," "Message…," "How can I help?" | Type your question and press **Enter** (or the ↑ arrow) |
| ➕ **Next to the box:** a **+** or 📎 | Add files and photos | Upload a picture, PDF or document for the AI to look at |
| 🎙️ **Microphone / waveform icon** | Voice | Talk instead of type (more in [chapter 9](09-voice-photos-and-files.md)) |
| ⬅️ **Left side (or ☰ menu on phones)** | Your chat history | Reopen old conversations, start a **New chat** |
| 🔽 **Top:** model picker | "Fast / Thinking," model names | Usually leave it on the default. Switch to "thinking" for tricky problems |
| 👤 **Your profile picture** | Settings | Privacy, memory, personalization, subscription |

> [!NOTE]
> **📌 Apps change their looks all the time**
> Buttons move and get renamed every few months. If something here doesn't match your screen exactly, look for the
> same *idea* nearby, or simply ask the AI: *"Where do I find the settings for memory in this app?"* It usually knows. 😄

## 💬 Step 3: Say hello (your first 10 messages)

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Here are ten fun, useful things to type so you can see what the AI can do. Copy them, change them, make them yours.

</details>

Type these one at a time (or change the details to fit your life). Read each answer and notice how it responds:

1. **Say hi and set the tone:**
   > *"Hi! I'm brand new to AI chatbots. Can you tell me, in simple words, five things you can help me with?"*
2. **Explain something you've always wondered:**
   > *"Why is the sky blue? Explain it like I'm 12."*
3. **Get everyday help:**
   > *"I have eggs, spinach, cheese and some leftover rice. What can I make for dinner in 20 minutes?"*
4. **Now ask a follow-up (no need to repeat yourself!):**
   > *"I don't have spinach after all. What can I use instead?"*
5. **Write something for you:**
   > *"Write a short, warm text message wishing my friend Sam good luck at her job interview tomorrow."*
6. **Change the style:**
   > *"Make it funnier and add an emoji or two."*
7. **Plan something:**
   > *"Help me plan a relaxing Saturday at home with my two kids, ages 6 and 9, on a rainy day. Low cost."*
8. **Learn something:**
   > *"Teach me three useful phrases in Italian for ordering at a café, with how to pronounce them."*
9. **Understand something confusing:**
   > *"What's the difference between a credit score and a credit report? Keep it short."*
10. **Ask about the AI itself:**
    > *"What are you not good at, and how should I double-check your answers?"*

**Notice:** you talked to it the way you'd talk to a helpful person. No special codes, no magic words. That's the whole
secret, and [Prompting 101](06-prompting-101.md) shows how to make your requests even better.

## 🔘 Step 4: The buttons that matter

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Under each answer are little buttons: copy it, ask for a new version, or give it a thumbs up or down. You can also fix
your own message if you made a typo.

</details>

Hover over (or long-press) an answer and you'll see small icons. Here's what they do:

| Icon | Name | When to use it |
|---|---|---|
| 📋 | **Copy** | Paste the answer into an email, document or text |
| 🔄 | **Regenerate / Try again** | Get a different version of the same answer |
| ✏️ | **Edit** (on *your* message) | Fix a typo or rephrase your question; the AI answers again from that point |
| 👍 👎 | **Feedback** | Tell the company what was good or bad (optional) |
| 🔊 | **Read aloud** | Hear the answer spoken |
| 🔗 / ⤴️ | **Share** | Create a link to the conversation (careful: anyone with the link can see it) |
| ➕ / ✍️ | **New chat** | Start fresh on a new topic |

> [!TIP]
> **💡 One topic per chat**
> Start a **new chat for each new topic** (dinner ideas in one, the job cover letter in another). It keeps answers
> focused, and your history turns into a tidy, searchable library of your conversations.

## 🔁 Step 5: Keep the conversation going

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

The magic is in the back-and-forth. If the answer isn't quite right, just tell it what to change, like you would with a
friend.

</details>

The biggest beginner mistake is treating AI like a search engine: one question, one answer, done. The real power is the
**conversation**. When an answer is *almost* right, don't start over; just steer:

| If the answer is… | Say… |
|---|---|
| Too long | *"Shorter, please. Just the key points."* |
| Too complicated | *"Explain that more simply, with an everyday example."* |
| Too generic | *"Make it specific to me: I live in a small apartment and work nights."* |
| Missing something | *"You forgot that I'm allergic to nuts."* |
| Not the right tone | *"More casual and friendly."* / *"More professional."* |
| Just okay | *"Give me 3 better options."* |
| Confusing | *"I don't understand the second step. Can you walk me through it?"* |

You'll master this in [Prompting 102](07-prompting-102.md).

## 🗂️ Step 6: Find, rename and delete your chats

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Your old chats are saved in a list, like old text conversations. You can reopen them, rename them, or delete them.

</details>

- **Find old chats:** the sidebar or ☰ menu lists them, newest first. Most apps have a 🔍 **search** too.
- **Rename:** hover over a chat (or long-press on phones) → **Rename**. "Italy trip ideas 🇮🇹" beats "New chat (3)."
- **Delete:** same menu → **Delete**. Good for anything personal you don't want kept around.
- **Temporary / private chats:** most assistants offer an incognito-style chat that isn't saved to your history or
  used for memory. Look for a 👻 ghost or "temporary chat" icon near the top. Handy for sensitive questions.

## 🩹 Step 7: When something looks odd

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Sometimes the app hiccups: it stops mid-sentence or says there's an error. That's normal. Usually trying again fixes it.

</details>

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| The answer **stops mid-sentence** | Type *"continue"* or press **Regenerate** |
| "**Something went wrong**" / network error | Wait a few seconds and try again; check your internet; refresh the page |
| "**You've reached your limit**" | Free plans have daily or hourly limits. Wait until the time shown, switch to a smaller model, or try another assistant |
| "**I can't help with that**" | Rephrase with context about *why* you're asking (see [chapter 2](02-how-ai-chatbots-work.md)) |
| The answer is **clearly wrong** | Tell it! *"That's not right. The event was in 2019."* It will usually correct itself |
| It **answers in the wrong language** | *"Please reply in English."* (or set your language in settings) |
| It **keeps getting worse** | Start a new chat |

More fixes live in the [Troubleshooting FAQ](../appendices/c-troubleshooting-faq.md).

## 🎯 Key takeaways

- Getting started is as easy as signing up for email: **open the official site or app, sign in, type.**
- Every chatbot shares the same layout: **message box, attachments, voice, chat history, settings.**
- Talk to it **like a helpful person**. No special language needed.
- The real power is in **follow-ups**: steer the answer instead of starting over.
- **Copy, regenerate, edit and new chat** are the four buttons you'll use constantly.
- Glitches are normal: **retry, rephrase, or start fresh.**

## 🧠 Check yourself

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 1. The AI's answer is good but way too long. What do you do?</summary>

Just reply *"Shorter, please"* (or *"Just the key points"*). No need to start over. Follow-ups are the secret.

</details>

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 2. You notice a typo in the question you just sent. What's the neatest fix?</summary>

Use the ✏️ **Edit** button on your message, fix it, and send. The AI answers again from that point.

</details>

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 3. Why is "one topic per chat" a good habit?</summary>

It keeps the AI **focused** (no confusion from earlier topics) and makes your chat history an **organized,
searchable** library.

</details>

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 4. An app called "AI Chat GPT Assistant Pro" wants a weekly subscription. Safe?</summary>

Be suspicious. Check the **developer name**: official apps come from the actual companies (OpenAI, Google, Anthropic,
Microsoft, Meta…). Lookalike apps often charge sneaky fees.

</details>

> [!TIP]
> **🎮 Try this**
> Have a **three-message conversation** about something real in your life this week (a meal, a message you need to
> send, a plan). Use at least one follow-up like *"make it shorter"* or *"give me 3 options."* Then **rename** the chat
> so you can find it later. Congratulations, you're officially an AI user! 🎉
