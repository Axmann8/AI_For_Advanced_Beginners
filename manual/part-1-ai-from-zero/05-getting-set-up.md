# 05 · Getting Set Up: Accounts, Apps & Plans 📲

> ⏱️ 9 min read · 🎯 Complete beginners · 🧰 Needs: your chosen assistant, 10 minutes in its settings

**A few minutes of setup makes every future conversation better.** This chapter covers where to use your assistant
(phone, computer or browser), how to keep your account secure, the five settings worth checking on day one, how to tell
the AI about yourself so answers fit *your* life, and a plain-English guide to free vs. paid plans so you never pay for
something you don't need.

<details class="eli5" open>
<summary>🧸 ELI5: This chapter in 30 seconds</summary>

Setting up your AI is like moving into a new room: put the lock on the door (a safe password), put your name on it
(tell the AI a little about you), and decide what gets kept and what gets thrown away (privacy settings). Then you can
decide later whether you want the bigger room (a paid plan).

</details>

## 📱 Phone, computer or browser?

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

You can use AI on your phone, in a program on your computer, or on a website. They all share the same chats, so use
whichever is handy.

</details>

Your account works everywhere, and your chats sync between devices. Most people use two of these:

| Where | Best for | How to get it |
|---|---|---|
| 📱 **Phone app** | Voice chats on a walk, snapping photos ("what's this plant?"), quick questions | App Store / Google Play: search the assistant's name, check the developer |
| 💻 **Desktop app** (Mac/Windows) | Everyday work, keyboard shortcuts, dropping in files | Download from the official website (chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com, etc.) |
| 🌐 **Website** | Using any computer, including work or library computers | Just go to the web address and sign in |
| 🧩 **Built into things you use** | Gemini in Gmail/Android, Copilot in Windows/Word, Meta AI in WhatsApp | Already there. Look for the sparkle ✨ or assistant icon |

> [!TIP]
> **💡 Put it one tap away**
> Add the app to your phone's home screen dock or widget area. Many apps also have a **lock-screen widget** or an
> **Action Button** / side-button shortcut to launch voice mode instantly. The easier it is to reach, the more you'll
> use it.

## 🔑 Lock the door: account security

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Your AI account may hold lots of your personal chats, so protect it like your email: a strong password and a second
"are you really you?" check.

</details>

Your chat history can contain a surprising amount about your life, so protect the account:

1. **Use "Sign in with Google/Apple/Microsoft"** *or* a **strong, unique password** (a password manager makes this easy).
   Passkeys, if offered, are even better.
2. **Turn on two-step verification (2FA)** in the account or security settings. It sends a code to your phone when
   someone logs in from a new device.
3. **Don't share your account** with others. Most assistants offer family or team plans instead, and shared accounts
   mix up memory and history.
4. **Log out** on shared or public computers (and use a temporary chat there).
5. **Watch out for fake login pages.** Always type the address yourself (chatgpt.com, gemini.google.com, claude.ai)
   rather than clicking links in emails or ads.

## ⚙️ Five settings to check on day one

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Before you start chatting a lot, peek at five switches: what the AI knows about you, what it remembers, whether your
chats help train it, how it looks, and how it sounds.

</details>

Open **Settings** (usually by tapping your profile picture or name). Look for these five things:

| # | Setting | Often called | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **About you** | Personalization, Custom instructions, "What should Claude know about you," Saved info | Add a few lines about yourself (see next section) |
| 2 | **Memory** | Memory, Reference chat history, Personal context | Decide: on (more personal answers) or off (more private). You can view and delete memories |
| 3 | **Data training** | "Improve the model for everyone," "Help improve," Activity | Choose whether your chats may be used to train future models |
| 4 | **Appearance & language** | Theme, Language | Dark mode if you like it; set your language |
| 5 | **Voice** | Voice | Pick a voice you enjoy listening to |

Where to find them in the popular apps (menus shift occasionally, so look for the *idea* if names differ):

=== "💬 ChatGPT"

    - **Profile picture → Settings → Personalization:** custom instructions ("What should ChatGPT know about you?"),
      personality style, and **Memory**.
    - **Settings → Data controls:** "Improve the model for everyone" on/off, export or delete your data.
    - **Temporary chat:** toggle at the top of a new chat.

=== "✨ Gemini"

    - **Settings (⚙️ or your profile) → Personal context / Saved info:** tell Gemini about yourself and manage what it
      remembers.
    - **Gemini Apps Activity:** control whether activity is saved and used to improve Google's AI, and for how long.
    - **Temporary chat:** available from the chat menu.

=== "🧡 Claude"

    - **Settings → Profile:** "What should Claude know about you?" and preferences for how it responds.
    - **Settings → Capabilities / Memory:** turn memory and chat search on or off.
    - **Settings → Privacy:** choose whether chats can be used to improve Claude. **Incognito chat:** the ghost icon.

=== "🪟 Copilot"

    - **Profile → Settings → Personalization / Memory:** what Copilot remembers about you.
    - **Privacy settings:** model training on your conversations and personalization. Your Microsoft account privacy
      dashboard shows and deletes activity.

=== "⚡ Grok · 🔎 Perplexity · 👓 Meta AI"

    - **Grok:** Settings → Data controls (training, memory, private chat). On X, also check **Settings → Privacy and
      safety → Grok & third-party collaborators**.
    - **Perplexity:** Settings → Personalization (tell it about you) and AI data retention.
    - **Meta AI:** the Meta AI app → Settings → Memory, and your Meta **Privacy Center** for how AI uses your data.

## 👤 Tell it about you (it makes a huge difference)

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

If the AI knows a few things about you, like where you live and what you like, its answers fit you much better, the way
a friend who knows you gives better advice than a stranger.

</details>

The **"about you" / custom instructions** box is the single biggest upgrade for beginners. Whatever you write there is
quietly included in every conversation. Here's a template to copy and adapt:

```text
About me:
- I'm Alex, 42, living in Leeds, UK. I work as a school administrator.
- Family: partner and two kids (8 and 11). We have a dog called Biscuit.
- I'm new to AI and not very technical.
- I cook most evenings; we eat mostly vegetarian.
- Use metric units and British spelling.

How to answer:
- Be warm and encouraging, and get to the point.
- Use short paragraphs and bullet points.
- If something is important to check (money, health, legal), tell me.
- Ask me a question if my request is unclear.
```

**Keep it to things you're comfortable sharing.** Skip exact addresses, ID numbers and anything sensitive. You can
change it any time, and a good test is to ask afterwards: *"What do you know about me?"*

> [!NOTE]
> **📌 Instructions vs. memory**
> **Custom instructions** are what *you* write deliberately. **Memory** is what the AI notices and saves on its own
> ("Alex is vegetarian"). Check your memory list now and then and delete anything wrong or too personal.

## 💳 Free vs. paid plans, in plain English

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

The free version is like a library card: great and free, but with limits. Paying is like a membership: more books, the
newest ones, and some extra rooms. Start free.

</details>

Every big assistant has tiers. The names change often, but the pattern is the same:

| Tier | What you get | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|
| 🆓 **Free** | The assistant, with daily limits on the smartest model and extras | Everyone starting out. Honestly enough for many people forever |
| 💰 **Lite / Go / Plus** (in some countries) | Higher limits at a lower price | Regular users who keep hitting free limits |
| ⭐ **Standard paid** (about the price of a streaming service) | Much higher limits, best models, voice, images, research tools | Daily users, people using AI for work |
| 🚀 **Premium** (several times more) | Very high limits, the most powerful models, early features | Heavy professional use |
| 🏢 **Business / Team / Enterprise** | Admin controls, stronger privacy guarantees, shared workspaces | Companies |

Current plan names for the popular ones (they rename things often!):

| Assistant | Free | Paid tiers you'll see |
|---|---|---|
| 💬 ChatGPT | Free | Go · Plus · Pro · Business/Enterprise |
| ✨ Gemini | Free | Google AI Plus (some countries) · Google AI Pro · Google AI Ultra (bundled with Google One storage) |
| 🧡 Claude | Free | Pro · Max · Team/Enterprise |
| 🪟 Copilot | Free | Included with Microsoft 365 Personal, Family and Premium plans |
| ⚡ Grok | Free | SuperGrok tiers (and access through X Premium) |
| 🔎 Perplexity | Free | Pro · Max · Enterprise |
| 🌬️ Le Chat | Free | Pro · Team · Enterprise |
| 👓 Meta AI | Free | Meta One subscriptions add more AI creation |

For exact current prices, check the company's pricing page. They change, and they differ by country.

## 🧾 Paying smart: subscriptions without surprises

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

If you do pay, set a reminder before any free trial ends, and know where to cancel. That way you never pay for
something you forgot about.

</details>

- **Where you subscribe is where you cancel.** Subscribed in the iPhone app? Cancel in your **Apple ID subscriptions**.
  Android? **Google Play → Subscriptions**. On the website? The assistant's **Settings → Subscription / Billing**.
- **Set a calendar reminder** two days before any free trial ends.
- **One at a time.** Paying for three assistants is rarely worth it. Upgrade the one you use most.
- **Check bundles:** Google AI plans include cloud storage; Microsoft 365 includes Office apps; some phone carriers and
  new phones include AI subscriptions. You might already have one!
- **Monthly first.** Try a month before committing to a yearly plan.

## 👨‍👩‍👧 Families, kids and shared devices

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Everyone should have their own account so their chats and memories stay separate, and kids should use AI with a
grown-up's guidance.

</details>

- **Separate accounts for each person.** Memory and history are personal; sharing an account mixes everything up.
- **Age rules:** most assistants require users to be 13+ (with parental permission until 18) or 18+. Several offer
  teen accounts and parental controls, and Google offers Gemini for supervised kids' accounts through Family Link.
- **Family plans** exist for some (Microsoft 365 Family, Google One family sharing for Google AI plans).
- **Shared computers:** use a temporary chat, and log out.

The full guide for parents is in [Parents, Teachers & Students](../part-11-ai-for-life-and-work/98-parents-teachers-and-students.md).

## ✅ Your setup checklist

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Tick these off and you're all set up, safe and ready.

</details>

- [ ] Official app installed on my phone (and/or desktop), launched from the home screen
- [ ] Strong password or "Sign in with…", plus two-step verification
- [ ] "About me" / custom instructions filled in
- [ ] Memory: on or off, my choice. I know where to review it
- [ ] Training on my chats: my choice
- [ ] I know how to start a **temporary / incognito** chat
- [ ] I'm on the free plan (for now) and know where I'd cancel if I upgrade

## 🎯 Key takeaways

- Use the **phone app** for voice and photos, the **desktop app or website** for longer work. Chats sync everywhere.
- **Secure the account:** strong password or sign-in provider, plus **two-step verification**.
- Check **five settings**: about you, memory, training, appearance, voice.
- **Custom instructions** are the biggest beginner upgrade: a few lines about you and how you like answers.
- **Start free.** Upgrade one assistant only when you keep hitting limits; cancel where you subscribed.

## 🧠 Check yourself

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 1. What's the difference between custom instructions and memory?</summary>

**Custom instructions** are what *you* deliberately write about yourself and how you want answers. **Memory** is what
the AI saves on its own from your chats. Review both now and then.

</details>

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 2. You subscribed through the iPhone app. Where do you cancel?</summary>

In your **Apple ID subscriptions** (Settings → your name → Subscriptions), not on the company's website.

</details>

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 3. Should your whole family share one AI account to save money?</summary>

Better not: memory and history get mixed up, and privacy suffers. Use **separate accounts** (and family plans where
available).

</details>

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 4. You're using a library computer. What two things should you do?</summary>

Use a **temporary / incognito chat** and **log out** when you're done.

</details>
