# 08 · The Beginner's Prompt Cookbook: 101 Prompts for Everyday Life 🍳

> ⏱️ 20 min browse · 🎯 Complete beginners (and anyone who wants ideas) · 🧰 Needs: any AI assistant

**Not sure what to ask? Start here.** 101 ready-to-use prompts for real life: home, food, money, work, learning,
writing, health, travel, fun, tech help and tricky conversations. Copy one, swap the **[bits in brackets]** for your
own details, and send. Each one is a little lesson in good prompting, too. 🍽️

<details class="eli5" open>
<summary>🧸 ELI5: This chapter in 30 seconds</summary>

This is a recipe book, but for questions. Find something you need help with, copy the question, change the words in
[brackets] to fit your life, and give it to your AI. Easy peasy.

</details>

> [!TIP]
> **💡 How to use this cookbook**

## 🎯 Key takeaways

- You don't need to invent prompts from scratch: **copy, customize the [brackets], send**.
- Notice the pattern in every good prompt: **task + context + details + format + tone** ([Prompting 101](06-prompting-101.md)).
- **Remove private numbers** before pasting bills and letters.
- Health, money and legal prompts are for **understanding and preparing**. Confirm with a professional.
- Want more? The [Prompt Library](../appendices/d-prompt-library.md) has 250 more, including advanced ones.

## 🧠 Check yourself

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 1. Before pasting your electricity bill into an AI, what should you remove?</summary>

Your **account number, address, and any payment details**. The AI only needs the charges and dates to explain it.

</details>

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 2. What's prompt #3's clever trick ("ask me questions one at a time")?</summary>

The AI **interviews you**, so the help fits your real situation instead of being generic.

</details>

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 3. Can you use AI to understand your blood test results?</summary>

Yes, to **understand the terms and prepare questions**. Always discuss your actual results and decisions with your
doctor.

</details>
