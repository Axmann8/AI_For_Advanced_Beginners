# 12 · AI Everywhere: Phones, Speakers, Cars, Glasses & Browsers 📱

> ⏱️ 14 min read · 🎯 Complete beginners · 🧰 Needs: your phone (and whatever else you own!)

**You don't always need to open a chatbot app: AI is now built into the phone in your pocket, the speaker in your
kitchen, your web browser, your car and even glasses.** This chapter is a friendly tour of the AI already waiting on
your devices, how to switch it on, and the handiest things to ask it. Chances are you own more AI than you realize. 😄

<details class="eli5" open>
<summary>🧸 ELI5: This chapter in 30 seconds</summary>

AI isn't just in one app anymore. It lives in your phone (Siri, Gemini), your speaker (Alexa), your computer (Copilot),
your web browser and even your car. You can just press a button or say a wake word and ask for help, wherever you are.

</details>

## 🍎 On iPhone, iPad and Mac

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Apple devices have a helper called Apple Intelligence and a much smarter Siri. You can also add apps like ChatGPT,
Gemini or Claude.

</details>

Apple's AI is called **Apple Intelligence**, and it powers a rebuilt, much more capable **Siri** (the big upgrade
arrived with iOS 27 in September 2026, using Google's Gemini models behind the scenes alongside Apple's own). Handy
things it does on supported devices:

| Feature | What it does | Try it |
|---|---|---|
| 🗣️ **Siri** | More natural conversation, understands what's on your screen and your personal context (messages, photos, calendar) | *"Siri, when is my mum's flight landing?"* |
| ✍️ **Writing Tools** | Rewrite, proofread or summarize text anywhere you type | Select text → **Writing Tools** |
| 📸 **Visual Intelligence** | Point the camera to identify things, translate or look things up | Press the Camera Control button or use the shortcut |
| 🧹 **Clean Up** in Photos | Remove distracting objects from photos | Photos → Edit → Clean Up |
| 🎨 **Image Playground & Genmoji** | Make fun images and custom emoji | Type an idea in the emoji keyboard |
| 🔔 **Summaries** | Short summaries of notifications, emails and long messages | Turn on in Settings → Notifications |
| 🌍 **Live Translation** | Translation in Messages, FaceTime and calls | Turn on in the app's settings |

**Setup:** Settings → **Apple Intelligence & Siri**. Features depend on your device model, language and country.

**Adding a full assistant app:** for deeper conversations, install **ChatGPT**, **Gemini** or **Claude** from the App
Store. You can put them on your Home Screen, Lock Screen widget or the **Action Button**, and use them with Siri
Shortcuts.

## 🤖 On Android phones

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Android phones have Gemini built in. Hold the power button or say "Hey Google" and ask anything, even about what's on
your screen.

</details>

On most Android phones, **Gemini** has replaced the old Google Assistant:

- **Open it:** long-press the power button, swipe from a bottom corner, or say *"Hey Google."*
- **Ask about your screen:** open Gemini while looking at something and tap *"Ask about screen"*: *"Summarize this
  article,"* *"What's this restaurant's rating?"*
- **Gemini Live:** talk naturally, and share your camera or screen.
- **Circle to Search:** long-press the home bar and circle anything on screen to search or translate it. Magical for
  "what's that jacket?" 🔍
- **Apps:** Gemini can work with Google Maps, Gmail, Calendar, Keep, YouTube and more: *"Add the dentist appointment in
  this email to my calendar."*

**Samsung Galaxy phones** add **Galaxy AI**: live call translation, Note Assist (summaries), Photo Assist edits, and
more, with Gemini built in. **Pixel phones** add extras like Magic Editor and Call Screen.

## 🪟 On Windows computers

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Windows has Copilot built in. Press the Copilot key or click its icon to ask for help, even about what's on your screen.

</details>

- **Copilot app:** press the **Copilot key** (on newer keyboards), click the taskbar icon, or say *"Hey Copilot"* if
  you've turned it on.
- **Copilot Vision:** share your screen or an app window and ask *"How do I do this in Excel?"* or *"Which of these
  settings should I change?"*
- **In Microsoft 365** (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook): Copilot drafts, summarizes and analyzes when included in your
  plan. See [Google Workspace & Microsoft 365 AI](../part-6-ai-in-your-apps/55-google-and-microsoft-ai.md).
- **Copilot+ PCs** add on-device features like improved Windows search and "Click to Do" actions.

Any assistant works on Windows too: ChatGPT, Claude and others have desktop apps.

## 🌐 In your web browser

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Some web browsers now have an AI helper in a sidebar that can read the page you're on, summarize it, or even click
around to help you do things.

</details>

Browsers are becoming AI-powered, with a sidebar that can read the page you're looking at:

| Browser / add-on | AI inside | Great for |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Chrome** | **Gemini in Chrome** (rolling out by country and plan) | "Summarize this page," "compare these products across my tabs" |
| **Microsoft Edge** | **Copilot** in the sidebar | Summaries, comparisons, writing help |
| **Comet** (Perplexity) | An AI-first browser, free to download | Research, shopping comparisons, having the assistant do web tasks |
| **Claude in Chrome** (extension) | Claude in a side panel that can navigate pages with you | Multi-step web chores (paid plans) |
| **ChatGPT** | Agent mode browses websites for you *inside* ChatGPT | "Find three hotels near the venue under $150 and compare them" |
| **Brave, Opera, Arc and others** | Built-in assistants (Leo, Aria and more) | Private or quick page summaries |

> [!WARNING]
> **⚠️ Agents that click for you: go slowly**
> AI that can click buttons and fill forms is powerful but still makes mistakes, and malicious web pages can try to
> trick it. Watch what it does, approve purchases yourself, and don't let it loose logged into your bank. More in
> [Computer Use & Browser Agents](../part-7-building-with-ai/71-computer-use-and-browser-agents.md).

## 🔊 Smart speakers and your home

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Speakers like Alexa and Google's now use new AI, so they understand you better and can help with more, like planning
dinner or controlling your lights with normal sentences.

</details>

- **Alexa+** (Amazon): the new AI-powered Alexa is far more conversational. It can plan, remember preferences, order
  groceries, book things and control smart home devices with natural requests (*"It's too bright in here and I'm
  cold"*). In the US it's included with Prime (or available as a standalone subscription), works on Echo devices, the
  Alexa app and **Alexa.com**.
- **Google Home with Gemini:** Gemini is replacing Google Assistant on Nest speakers and displays, bringing more natural
  conversation and smarter home control (rolling out by country).
- **HomePod / Siri:** the new Siri brings more natural home requests to Apple devices.

**Great kitchen requests:** *"Set timers for the pasta and the garlic bread,"* *"What can I substitute for buttermilk?,"*
*"Add oat milk to the shopping list,"* *"Tell the kids dinner's ready"* (on speakers that support announcements).

## 🚗 In the car

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Many cars now let you talk to an AI while driving, so you can ask questions or send messages without touching your
phone.

</details>

- **Android Auto** brings **Gemini** for hands-free messages, directions and questions.
- **Apple CarPlay** uses **Siri**.
- Many newer cars have built-in assistants powered by big AI models (several brands use ChatGPT, Gemini or their own),
  and **Tesla** cars include **Grok**.
- **Voice mode** in any assistant app works too, for talk-radio-style learning on long drives.

> [!CAUTION]
> **🛑 Eyes on the road**
> Use voice only while driving. Never read or type AI answers behind the wheel.

## 👓 Glasses, watches and gadgets

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Some glasses have a camera and speakers with AI inside, so you can ask "what am I looking at?" hands-free.

</details>

- **Ray-Ban Meta and Oakley Meta glasses** have **Meta AI** built in: *"Hey Meta, what's this building?"*, translate
  a sign, capture photos, get help hands-free. Some newer models add a small display.
- **Smartwatches** (Apple Watch, Galaxy Watch, Pixel Watch) let you ask Siri or Gemini from your wrist.
- **Earbuds** now support live translation on several phones.
- More AI glasses from Google, Samsung and others are arriving. Expect this category to grow quickly.

## 💬 In the apps you already chat in

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

You can talk to Meta AI right inside WhatsApp or Instagram, just like chatting with a friend.

</details>

- **WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger:** chat with **Meta AI** directly, or mention **@Meta AI** in a group chat to ask
  it something for everyone (*"@Meta AI suggest a restaurant for 8 near King's Cross"*).
- **X (Twitter):** **Grok** is built in; tap the Grok icon or ask about a post.
- **Telegram, Slack, Discord, Teams:** many AI bots and assistants live there too (see [Chat Apps &
  Bots](../part-6-ai-in-your-apps/59-chat-apps-and-bots.md)).

## 🎯 Key takeaways

- **iPhone/Mac:** Apple Intelligence and the new Siri, plus any assistant app you like.
- **Android:** Gemini (power button or "Hey Google"), Circle to Search, and Galaxy AI on Samsung.
- **Windows:** Copilot (the Copilot key, Copilot Vision, and Microsoft 365 apps).
- **Browsers, speakers, cars, glasses and messaging apps** all have AI built in now. Try what you already own first.
- Be careful with **agents that click and buy**, and always **drive hands-free**.

## 🧠 Check yourself

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 1. On an Android phone, how do you ask about an article you're reading?</summary>

Open **Gemini** (long-press the power button or say "Hey Google") and tap **"Ask about screen"**, or use **Circle to
Search**.

</details>

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 2. Where can you use Meta AI without installing anything new?</summary>

Inside **WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger** (and on Ray-Ban Meta glasses).

</details>

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 3. Your browser's AI agent offers to finish a purchase for you. What's the safe approach?</summary>

Let it research and fill the cart, but **review and approve the purchase yourself**, and avoid letting agents act while
logged into your bank.

</details>

> [!TIP]
> **🎮 Try this**
> **The device scavenger hunt:** find the AI on **every** device you own today: phone, computer, browser, speaker, TV,
> car. Ask each one the same question (*"What's a fun fact about today's date?"*) and notice which one you like best.
> You might discover a helper that's been hiding in plain sight. 🕵️
