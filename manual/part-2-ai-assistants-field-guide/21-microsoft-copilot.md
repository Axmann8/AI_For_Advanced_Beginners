# 21 · Microsoft Copilot: The Complete Guide 🪟

> ⏱️ 8 min read · 🎯 Windows, Office and Outlook users · 🧰 Needs: a Microsoft account (Outlook, Xbox or Office login)

**Copilot is Microsoft's AI assistant, and it lives where millions of people already work: Windows, the Edge browser,
Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Teams.** In 2026 Microsoft merged its consumer Copilot app and its Microsoft 365
Copilot app into one **Microsoft Copilot** app, so the same assistant now helps with everyday questions *and* your
documents and email. This chapter shows you what's where, what changed, and the best ways to use it.

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Copilot is Microsoft's AI assistant, built into Windows, the Edge browser and Microsoft 365 apps like Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook. If you use a Windows PC or Office for work or school, it's often the most convenient assistant available.

- **Free features:** chat, voice, Copilot Vision (screen and camera help) and image creation.
- **With Microsoft 365:** Copilot inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook.
- **Advanced:** Researcher and Analyst agents on premium and business plans.

</details>

## 🪟 Quick facts

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Copilot is made by Microsoft and uses models from OpenAI and Microsoft. It's available as an app on Windows, Mac and phones, on the web, in Edge, and inside Microsoft 365 apps.

</details>

| | |
|---|---|
| **Made by** | Microsoft (Microsoft AI), using OpenAI's and Microsoft's own models |
| **Where** | The **Microsoft Copilot** app (Windows, Mac, iPhone, Android, web) · the Copilot key on Windows · **Edge** · Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, OneNote, Teams |
| **Free?** | Yes for chat, voice, vision and images; Office-app features need a Microsoft 365 plan |
| **Best at** | Working inside Windows and Microsoft 365, screen help with Copilot Vision, everyday answers |
| **Watch out for** | Several consumer features were retired in August 2026; features differ between personal and work accounts |

> [!NOTE]
> **📌 What changed in August 2026**
> Microsoft combined its consumer and business Copilot apps into one app (rolling out from mid-August on mobile and web,
> mid-September on Windows and Mac). Along the way it **retired** several consumer features: **Group Chats, Copilot
> Podcasts, Copilot Labs and consumer Deep Research**. The animated **Mico** character left standard voice mode (it
> lives on in the **Learn Live** tutoring experience). Deep research lives on as **Researcher** for Microsoft 365
> Premium subscribers. If older guides mention those features, that's why they're missing.

## 🚪 Getting started

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Getting started with Copilot is quick.

1. Press the Copilot key or taskbar icon on Windows, or open the Copilot app or website.
2. Sign in with your Microsoft account (or your work account).
3. Type or speak your first question.

</details>

1. **Open Copilot:** press the **Copilot key** (or the taskbar icon) on Windows, open the **Microsoft Copilot** app on
   your phone or Mac, or go to the Copilot website.
2. **Sign in** with your **Microsoft account** (personal) or your **work/school account**. Signing in saves your
   history and unlocks more.
3. **Personalize:** Settings → **Personalization / Memory**: let Copilot remember your preferences, or turn it off.
4. **Voice:** optionally turn on **"Hey Copilot"** on Windows for hands-free use.

## 💳 Plans in plain English

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Copilot chat is free with a Microsoft account. Using Copilot inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook at home requires a Microsoft 365 subscription; business plans add work-data features. The table compares them.

</details>

| Plan | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Free** (Microsoft account) | Chat, voice, Copilot Vision, image creation, web answers |
| **Microsoft 365 Personal / Family** | Copilot inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and OneNote (with monthly AI usage), plus the Office apps and cloud storage |
| **Microsoft 365 Premium** | Microsoft's most complete personal plan (up to six people): the highest Copilot usage, **Researcher** and **Analyst** agents, and the newest features |
| **Microsoft 365 Copilot** (business) | Copilot across your organization's email, files, meetings and chats, with enterprise data protection |

## 💬 Everyday chat, voice and vision

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Copilot supports typed and spoken conversation, file and photo uploads, and **Copilot Vision**, which can look at your screen or camera and guide you through what you're seeing.

</details>

- **Chat:** ask anything, and upload files and photos.
- **Voice:** tap the microphone for a spoken conversation.
- **Copilot Vision:** share your **screen** on Windows (or your **camera** on mobile) and ask *"How do I turn off these
  notifications?"*, *"What's the best move in this game?"* or *"Which of these laptops is better value?"* It highlights
  where to click. It's brilliant for tech help.
- **Image creation:** *"Create an image of…"* right in the chat.
- **Learn Live:** a tutoring mode that teaches step by step with questions and visuals.

## 📝 Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

With a Microsoft 365 plan, a Copilot button appears in each Office app. Use it to draft and rewrite in Word, write formulas and analyze data in Excel, build slides in PowerPoint and summarize or draft email in Outlook. The table has example prompts.

</details>

With a Microsoft 365 plan, look for the **Copilot** button in each app:

| App | Try this |
|---|---|
| **Word** | *"Draft a two-page proposal from these notes,"* "Rewrite this section to be more concise," "Summarize this document" |
| **Excel** | *"Add a column that calculates profit margin,"* "Highlight the top 10 customers," "What trends do you see?," "Explain this formula" |
| **PowerPoint** | *"Create a 10-slide presentation from this Word document,"* "Add a slide summarizing the budget," "Make the design more modern" |
| **Outlook** | *"Summarize this thread,"* "Draft a reply accepting the meeting but proposing Thursday," "Coach me on the tone of this email" |
| **OneNote** | *"Summarize my notes from this week and list the to-dos"* |
| **Teams** (work) | Meeting recaps, "what did I miss?," action items |

The merged Copilot app also lets you jump from a chat into these apps and your files. More in
[Google Workspace & Microsoft 365 AI](../part-6-ai-in-your-apps/55-google-and-microsoft-ai.md).

## 🌐 Copilot in Edge and Windows

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

In Edge, Copilot can summarize pages, compare products across tabs and draft text; Copilot Mode can also carry out tasks on websites. In Windows, Copilot can help find settings and files.

</details>

- **Edge:** open Copilot in the sidebar to summarize pages, compare products across tabs, or draft text. Edge's
  **Copilot Mode** turns the browser into an AI-first experience that can help with multi-step browsing tasks (with your
  permission).
- **Windows:** ask Copilot to explain settings, find files, or help with apps, and share your screen with Vision. On
  **Copilot+ PCs**, features like smarter Windows search and **Click to Do** (actions on whatever's on screen) run partly
  on-device.

## 🔬 Researcher and Analyst (Premium and business)

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Premium and business plans include two specialist agents. **Researcher** conducts multi-step research and writes a cited report; **Analyst** works through data and spreadsheets to find insights.

</details>

- **Researcher:** multi-step research across the web (and your work files, on business plans) that produces a detailed,
  cited report.
- **Analyst:** a data-focused agent that works through spreadsheets and data files, running analysis and explaining
  findings.

These are included with **Microsoft 365 Premium** and business Copilot licenses.

## 🍳 Step-by-step recipes

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

These four recipes walk through real tasks in Copilot step by step, from fixing a Windows setting with Vision to drafting in Office.

</details>

**Recipe 1: Fix a Windows annoyance with Vision**

1. Open Copilot → **share screen** → pick the Settings window.
2. *"My laptop keeps waking up at night. What should I change here?"* Follow the highlighted steps.

**Recipe 2: Budget spreadsheet in Excel**

1. Open your spending sheet → Copilot.
2. *"Categorize these transactions, total each category, and make a chart of where my money goes."*

**Recipe 3: Inbox rescue in Outlook**

1. Open a long thread → **Summarize**.
2. *"Draft a reply confirming I'll send the report by Friday and asking for the latest figures."*

**Recipe 4: From document to deck**

1. In PowerPoint → Copilot → *"Create a presentation from [document]."*
2. *"Make it 8 slides, simpler words, and add speaker notes."*

## 🔐 Privacy and settings

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

The table lists Copilot's key privacy settings, including memory, model training and history deletion, and where to find each one.

</details>

| Setting | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Memory / personalization** | Copilot → Settings | What Copilot remembers about you |

## 💡 Pro tips and limitations

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

These tips help you get more out of Copilot, followed by its current limitations so you know what to watch for.

</details>

**Pro tips**

- ⌨️ **Copilot key + Vision** is the fastest tech support you've ever had.
- 🏢 **At work, use your work account** in Copilot. It can then use your company's email, files and meetings securely.
- 📎 **Reference files** in prompts (in Microsoft 365 apps, type **/** to pick a file): *"Summarize /Q3 report and
  compare to /Q2 report."*

**Limitations**

- **Personal vs. work accounts** see different features, which can be confusing.
- Office-app features need a **Microsoft 365** subscription and have monthly usage limits.
- Microsoft **adds and retires features often**. The merged app is still evolving toward a bigger "super app."

## 🎯 Key takeaways

- Copilot is the natural choice for **Windows and Microsoft 365** users.
- **Copilot Vision** (screen and camera sharing) makes it a superb tech-help buddy.
- **Microsoft 365 plans** unlock Copilot in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and OneNote; **Premium** adds
  Researcher and Analyst.
- In **August 2026** consumer Group Chats, Podcasts, Labs and Deep Research were retired and the apps merged.
- Use your **work account** for work data; check privacy settings for personal use.
