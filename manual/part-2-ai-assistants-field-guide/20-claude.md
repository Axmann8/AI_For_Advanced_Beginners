# 20 · Claude: The Complete Guide 🧡

> ⏱️ 9 min read · 🎯 Beginners to advanced beginners · 🧰 Needs: a free Claude account (claude.ai)

**Claude is Anthropic's assistant, loved for writing that sounds human, careful thinking, and working brilliantly with
long documents.** It's also become a serious "get work done" tool: it creates real files (documents, spreadsheets,
slides), builds interactive mini-apps called Artifacts, connects to your apps, works through big tasks on your computer
with **Cowork**, and powers **Claude Code**, one of the most popular AI coding tools. This guide covers all of it.

<details class="eli5" open>
<summary>🧸 ELI5: This chapter in 30 seconds</summary>

Claude is a thoughtful AI helper that's especially good at reading and writing. Give it a long document and it
understands it; ask it to write and it sounds natural. It can also make things you can use, like documents,
spreadsheets and little apps, and it can even work on tasks on your computer while you do something else.

</details>

## 🚪 Getting started

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Sign up with your email, tell Claude about yourself, pick your privacy settings and start chatting.

</details>

1. **Sign up** at **claude.ai** or in the Claude app (maker: **Anthropic**). Email login sends a magic link (no
   password needed), or use "Continue with Google."
2. **Profile:** Settings → **Profile** → "What should Claude know about you?" and how you'd like responses.
3. **Styles:** choose a response style (Normal, Concise, Explanatory, Formal) or create your own from a writing sample.
4. **Capabilities:** Settings → **Capabilities** → turn on web search, memory, file creation and other features you
   want.
5. **Privacy:** Settings → **Privacy** → choose whether your chats can help improve Claude.

## 📁 Projects: context that sticks

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

A Project is a workspace where you put instructions and files once, and every chat in it remembers them.

</details>

**Projects** hold instructions and a knowledge base (files, documents, notes) for a topic:

1. Sidebar → **Projects** → **Create project**.
2. Add **instructions** (*"You're helping me run my bakery. Our tone is warm and cheeky; we're in Bristol; we care
   about local, seasonal ingredients."*).
3. Upload **files**: menus, price lists, past newsletters, a style guide. Projects can hold a lot, and Claude reads only
   what's relevant to each question.
4. Chat inside the project. Every conversation starts with that context.

Share projects with teammates on Team plans. Ideas: a book you're writing, a course, your business, a big home project.

## 🧩 Artifacts and file creation

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Claude can build things you can see and use right in the chat, like a little game, a chart or a planner, and it can make
real Word, Excel and PowerPoint files.

</details>

- **Artifacts:** when you ask for something substantial (a document, a diagram, a chart, a web page or an interactive
  mini-app), Claude builds it in a panel beside the chat. You can edit, iterate, download or share it. Try: *"Make an
  interactive tool where I enter my monthly expenses and see a pie chart."* Artifacts can even use AI themselves, so you
  can build small AI-powered apps just by describing them.
- **Real files:** Claude can create and edit **Word documents, Excel spreadsheets (with working formulas), PowerPoint
  decks and PDFs**. *"Turn these notes into a 10-slide deck,"* *"Make a budget spreadsheet with monthly totals and a
  chart."*
- **Skills:** packages of instructions and resources that teach Claude a specific way of working (your company's
  document style, a data-analysis method). Claude loads them automatically when relevant; you can create your own.

## 🧠 Memory and search

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Claude can remember important things from your chats and search your past conversations, and you control what it keeps.

</details>

- **Memory:** Claude can remember context from past chats (your role, ongoing projects, preferences), across both chats
  and Cowork. View and edit what it remembers in Settings, and ask it to remember or forget things.
- **Chat search:** *"What did we decide about the garden layout last month?"* Claude can search your past
  conversations.
- **Incognito chats:** the 👻 icon starts a chat that isn't saved to memory or history.

## 🌐 Web search and Research

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Claude can look things up online with links to sources, and Research mode digs through many sources to write a thorough
report.

</details>

- **Web search:** turn it on in the tools menu; Claude searches when needed and cites sources.
- **Research:** for big questions, Research mode runs many searches (and can include your connected Gmail, Calendar and
  Drive), reasoning through them for several minutes to produce a thorough, cited report.

## 🔌 Connectors: Claude and your apps

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

You can connect Claude to apps like Gmail, Google Drive, Notion or Canva so it can read your stuff and do things for you.

</details>

Claude has a big **connector directory** (Settings → **Connectors**): Gmail, Google Calendar and Drive, Notion, Slack,
Canva, Figma, Asana, Linear, GitHub, Stripe and many more. Anthropic created **MCP** (Model Context Protocol), the open
standard most assistants now use for connecting AI to apps, so Claude supports it deeply, including custom connectors.

*"Check my calendar for next week and find a free two-hour slot for deep work,"* *"Summarize the Notion page about our
launch plan,"* *"Make a Canva poster from this text."* Deep dive: [MCP Explained](../part-4-mcp-and-connectors/38-mcp-explained.md)
and [Built-in Connectors](../part-4-mcp-and-connectors/41-built-in-connectors.md).

## 🤝 Cowork: Claude works on your computer

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Cowork is like giving Claude a desk next to yours: it can read the files you allow, do long jobs in the background, and
hand you finished work.

</details>

**Cowork** (paid plans) is Claude's "coworker" mode in the desktop app, and increasingly on web and mobile. You give it a
task and access to specific folders or apps, and it works through the job, multi-step and in the background, then
delivers finished work:

- *"Go through the receipts in my Downloads/receipts folder and make an expense spreadsheet by category."*
- *"Read these 12 customer interviews and write a report on the top five themes with quotes."*
- *"Every Monday, summarize last week's project notes into a status update."* (scheduled tasks)

You decide which folders and apps it can touch, and it asks before anything risky. It's like [Claude
Code](../part-7-building-with-ai/62-claude-code-masterclass.md) for non-coding knowledge work.

## 🌍 Claude in Chrome, Excel and Slack

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Claude also lives inside your web browser, Excel and Slack, so it can help right where you're working.

</details>

- **Claude in Chrome** (extension, paid plans): Claude in a side panel that sees the page and can navigate, click and
  fill forms with you (*"compare prices for this blender across these tabs"*). Supervise it and keep it away from
  sensitive accounts.
- **Claude in Excel** (and other Office add-ins on some plans): build and explain formulas, analyze sheets, fix errors
  in place.
- **Claude in Slack:** mention Claude in your workspace to summarize threads, draft replies or research.

## 🎙️ Voice and vision

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

You can talk with Claude out loud in the app, and show it photos, screenshots and documents.

</details>

- **Voice mode** (free on mobile): tap the voice icon for a spoken conversation. It's great for thinking out loud.
- **Photos, screenshots and PDFs:** Claude reads charts, handwriting, forms and long PDFs (including their images and
  tables) very well.
- **What it doesn't do:** Claude doesn't generate photos or videos. It makes diagrams, charts, SVG graphics and
  interactive visuals instead. Pair it with Gemini or ChatGPT for image creation.

## 💻 Claude Code

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Claude Code is Claude's coding helper. It can build and fix whole apps and websites, even for people who aren't
programmers.

</details>

**Claude Code** is Anthropic's coding agent, in the terminal, desktop app, web and code editors. It reads your project,
makes changes, runs tests and can work on tasks in the background. It's a favorite among developers and increasingly
among "vibe coders" building their first apps. Full guides: [The Claude Code
Masterclass](../part-7-building-with-ai/62-claude-code-masterclass.md) and [Power-Ups](../part-7-building-with-ai/63-claude-code-power-ups.md).

## 🍳 Step-by-step recipes

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Four real jobs you can do with Claude today.

</details>

**Recipe 1: Understand a long contract**

1. Upload the PDF.
2. *"Summarize this lease in plain English. Then list anything unusual, anything that could cost me money, and questions
   I should ask the landlord. Quote the relevant clauses."*

**Recipe 2: Build a personal mini-app**

1. *"Make an interactive habit tracker artifact: I can add habits, tick them off each day, and see a streak counter.
   Cheerful design."*
2. Iterate: *"Add a weekly view and confetti when I hit a 7-day streak."* 🎉

**Recipe 3: A writing partner that sounds like you**

1. Settings → Styles → **Create style** → upload two things you've written.
2. *"Using my style, write a newsletter intro about our spring menu."*

**Recipe 4: Spreadsheet from a mess**

1. Paste messy notes of your monthly spending.
2. *"Turn this into an Excel file with categories, monthly totals, a chart, and a formula that flags anything over
   budget."*

## 💡 Pro tips and limitations

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Clever tricks Claude fans know, and the things to watch out for.

</details>

**Pro tips**

- 📚 **Feed it long stuff:** entire reports, books, transcripts. Claude handles very long documents well. Ask it to
  quote sources inside your documents.
- 🎭 **Ask for honesty:** Claude is good at saying "I'm not sure," and it gets even better if you ask *"flag anything
  you're uncertain about."*
- 🧠 **Extended thinking:** for hard problems, turn on extended thinking (or ask it to "think carefully").
- 🧩 **Artifacts as tools:** build your own calculators, planners, quizzes and dashboards, then share them with a link.

**Limitations**

- **No photo or video generation.**
- **Free usage limits** can run out during long sessions; Pro and Max raise them a lot.
- Like all AI, it can make mistakes. Verify important facts.

## 🎯 Key takeaways

- Claude shines at **writing, long documents, careful reasoning** and **making real things**: files, Artifacts,
  mini-apps.
- **Projects** keep context; **Memory** and **chat search** carry it across conversations.
- **Connectors** (via MCP, which Anthropic created) link Claude to your apps.
- **Cowork, Claude in Chrome and Claude Code** let it work *for* you, supervised.
- Check **Privacy** settings and use **incognito** for sensitive chats.
