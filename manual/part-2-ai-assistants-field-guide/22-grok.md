# 22 · Grok: The Complete Guide ⚡

> ⏱️ 8 min read · 🎯 Beginners to advanced beginners · 🧰 Needs: a free Grok account (grok.com) or an X account

**Grok is the AI assistant from xAI, Elon Musk's AI company (now part of SpaceX), and it's built right into X (formerly
Twitter).** It stands out for real-time knowledge of what's happening on X, a casual and witty personality, strong
reasoning modes, and **Grok Imagine** for making images and videos. It's also been the most controversial of the big
assistants, so this guide covers both the fun features and the sensible precautions.

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Grok is the AI assistant from xAI (now part of SpaceX), available in its own app and built into X. It stands out for real-time awareness of posts on X, a distinctive personality, and image and video generation.

- **Core features:** chat, voice, web and X search, and several reasoning modes.
- **Creative tools:** Grok Imagine for images and short videos.
- **Use with care:** posts on X aren't always accurate, so check sources, and never create misleading images of real people.

</details>

## 🚪 Getting started

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Getting started with Grok is simple.

1. Sign up at grok.com or in the Grok app using X, Google, Apple or email (or tap the Grok icon in X).
2. Choose a mode (Auto is fine to start).
3. Ask your first question.

</details>

1. **Sign up** at **grok.com** or in the **Grok** app (maker: **xAI**). You can sign in with X, Google, Apple or email.
2. **Or use it inside X:** tap the **Grok** icon, or tap Grok on any post to ask about it (*"Explain this post,"* *"Is
   this true?"*).
3. **Customize:** Settings → **Customize** → tell Grok how to respond; choose a personality style.
4. **Pick a mode** (see below) and start chatting.

## 📰 Real-time X and web search

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Grok can search posts on X in real time as well as the web, which makes it useful for understanding trending topics and public reaction. Because posts can be inaccurate, check the sources it cites before relying on a claim.

</details>

Grok's signature skill is **live awareness of X**, plus web search:

- *"What are people saying about the new iPhone today? Summarize the main opinions."*
- *"Explain this trending hashtag and why it's controversial."*
- *"Find the original source for this viral claim."*
- **DeepSearch** (and similar research modes) runs many searches and writes a longer, cited answer.

> [!WARNING]
> **⚠️ Popular isn't the same as true**
> X posts can be wrong, one-sided or deliberately misleading, and Grok can repeat them. For news and facts, **check the
> cited sources** and reputable outlets ([When AI Gets It Wrong](../part-1-ai-from-zero/10-when-ai-gets-it-wrong.md)).

## 🎨 Grok Imagine: images and video

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Grok Imagine creates images and short videos with sound from text descriptions. Use it for creative projects, and never to create deceptive images of real people.

</details>

**Grok Imagine** (in the Grok app and on X) creates:

- 🖼️ **Images:** *"A cozy cabin in a snowy forest at night, warm windows, cinematic."*
- 🎬 **Short videos with sound**, from a description or by animating an image: *"Animate this: the snow starts
  falling and smoke rises from the chimney."*
- Several **styles and modes**, including quick "spicy"/adult settings restricted to verified adults.

> [!CAUTION]
> **🛑 Never edit real people's photos in hurtful ways**
> In early 2026, Grok's image editing was widely misused to create sexualized images of real people (including minors)
> without consent. That triggered investigations, court orders and new restrictions, including limiting editing of real
> people's photos and blocking revealing edits where they're illegal. Creating such images is harmful, and in many
> places it's a crime. Keep Grok Imagine for your own creations.

## 🎙️ Voice and companions

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Voice mode supports spoken conversation with a choice of voices and personality styles. Grok also offers animated "companion" characters; review their settings carefully, especially for younger users.

</details>

- **Voice mode:** tap the voice icon for a spoken conversation. Choose among voices and personality styles (from
  helpful assistant to storyteller or comedian).
- **Companions:** animated characters you can talk to. Fun for some adults, but think carefully about kids and teens
  using them, and keep an eye on how much time goes into AI companionship
  ([Staying Safe](../part-1-ai-from-zero/11-staying-safe-with-ai.md#-looking-after-yourself)).
- **In Tesla cars:** Grok is available as an in-car voice assistant in supported models.

## 🗂️ Projects, memory and more

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Grok supports Projects for organizing chats and files by topic, memory across conversations, and file uploads. xAI also runs Grokipedia, an AI-generated encyclopedia.

</details>

- **Projects:** group chats with files and instructions for a topic.
- **Memory:** Grok can remember preferences across chats; manage it in settings.
- **Files and images:** upload documents and photos for analysis.
- **Grokipedia:** xAI's AI-generated encyclopedia. Treat it like any AI output: interesting, but verify important facts
  with other sources.
- **For developers:** the xAI API offers Grok models, image and video generation, and voice APIs.

## 🔐 Privacy and settings

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

The table lists Grok's privacy settings, including whether your conversations and X posts are used for training, memory and private chats.

</details>

| Setting | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Data controls / training** | Grok → Settings → Data controls | Choose whether your conversations help improve the models |
| **X posts and Grok** | X → Settings → Privacy and safety → **Grok & third-party collaborators** | Stop your public X posts and interactions being used for training |
| **Memory** | Grok → Settings | Turn off or manage what's remembered |
| **Private chat** | 👻 icon in Grok | A chat that isn't saved |
| **Delete conversations** | Grok → Settings | Remove your history |

## 💡 Pro tips and limitations

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

These tips help you get more out of Grok, followed by its current limitations so you know when to double-check.

</details>

**Pro tips**

- 🧵 **Summarize a whole thread** on X: tap Grok on the first post.
- 🎭 **Set the personality** you like in Customize. It changes the vibe a lot.
- 🔁 **Compare modes:** ask the same hard question in Fast and Expert to see the difference.

**Limitations**

- It has produced **offensive or false outputs** in the past; xAI has adjusted it several times. Keep a critical eye.
- Real-time X content can be **unreliable**.
- **Kids and teens:** the looser style and companion features mean extra parental caution.

## 🎯 Key takeaways

- Grok is **xAI's assistant** (now part of SpaceX), built into **X**, with its own app and Tesla integration.
- Its strengths: **real-time X awareness**, a **witty personality**, **Expert/Heavy** reasoning and **Grok Imagine**
  for images and video.
- **Verify** claims from social media, and **never** use image tools on real people without consent.
- Check **Data controls** and your **X privacy settings** for training.
