# 23 · Perplexity: The Complete Guide 🔎

> ⏱️ 7 min read · 🎯 Beginners to advanced beginners · 🧰 Needs: nothing (an account saves your history; perplexity.ai)

**Perplexity calls itself an "answer engine": ask a question and you get a clear, written answer with numbered sources
you can click, like a search engine and a research assistant rolled into one.** It's the go-to tool for fact-finding,
comparisons, product research and staying on top of news, and its free **Comet** browser brings an AI assistant to every
web page. If you care about *where* an answer came from, this is your tool.

<details class="eli5" open>
<summary>🧸 ELI5: This chapter in 30 seconds</summary>

Perplexity is like a librarian who runs off, reads lots of websites for you, and comes back with a short answer plus
little numbered notes saying exactly where each fact came from. So you can check it! It's great for questions like
"which vacuum should I buy?" or "what happened in the news today?"

</details>

## 🚪 Getting started

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Go to perplexity.ai and just ask a question. Sign up if you want to save your searches.

</details>

1. Go to **perplexity.ai** or install the app (maker: **Perplexity AI**). You can ask questions without an account.
2. **Sign up** (email, Google or Apple) to save your **Library** of past searches.
3. **Personalize:** Settings → **Personalization** → tell it about you (location, interests, how you like answers).
4. Try the **Discover** tab for AI-summarized news tailored to your interests.

## 🧭 Search modes: quick, Pro, Research and Labs

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Perplexity has quick answers for easy questions, deeper searches for harder ones, a research mode that writes reports,
and Labs, which can even build spreadsheets and dashboards.

</details>

| Mode | What it does | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ⚡ **Search** (quick) | A fast answer with sources | *"What time does the Louvre open on Sundays?"* |
| 🧠 **Pro Search** | Breaks your question into steps, searches more deeply | *"Best budget robot vacuum for pet hair in 2026?"* |
| 🔬 **Research** (deep research) | Reads dozens of sources for a few minutes and writes a long, structured report | *"Heat pumps vs. gas boilers for a 1930s UK house: costs, grants, pros and cons"* |
| 🧪 **Labs** | Builds finished things: reports with charts, spreadsheets, dashboards, even simple web apps | *"Build a dashboard comparing the 10 largest EV makers by sales and range"* |

**Focus / sources:** you can steer where it searches: the web, **academic papers**, **social discussions**,
**finance data**, or your own files.

## 🔗 Reading sources like a pro

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Every fact has a little number. Tap it to see the website it came from, and check that the website really says that.

</details>

Every Perplexity answer has **numbered citations** [1][2][3]:

1. **Hover or tap** a number to see the source.
2. **Open** the ones behind important claims. Does the page actually say that?
3. **Check the source quality:** an official site or major publication beats a random blog or forum.
4. **Check dates:** is the info current?
5. Ask follow-ups: *"Which of these sources are most reliable?"* or *"Find a primary source for claim 3."*

This habit makes Perplexity one of the most **trustworthy** ways to use AI for facts
([When AI Gets It Wrong](../part-1-ai-from-zero/10-when-ai-gets-it-wrong.md)).

## 🗂️ Spaces: your research folders

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Spaces are folders for a topic, with your own instructions and files, so all your research on one thing stays together.

</details>

**Spaces** group searches by topic, with **custom instructions** and **uploaded files**:

1. Sidebar → **Spaces** → **Create a Space** (*"🏡 Buying our first home"*).
2. Add instructions (*"We're first-time buyers in Ohio, budget $300k, two kids. Prioritize official and local
   sources."*) and files (pre-approval letter details, listings).
3. Every search in the Space uses that context. Share it with your partner to research together.

## ☄️ Comet: the AI browser

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Comet is a web browser with an AI helper built in. It can explain the page you're on, compare things across tabs, and even
do web chores for you while you watch.

</details>

**Comet** is Perplexity's free web browser (Windows, Mac, iPhone, Android). It looks like Chrome (and imports your
bookmarks), but has an AI **assistant** on every page:

- *"Summarize this article and tell me what's missing from it."*
- *"Compare the prices and reviews of this blender across my open tabs."*
- *"Find me a table for four at an Italian restaurant near here on Friday at 7."* (the assistant navigates for you and
  asks before booking)
- *"Unsubscribe me from these three newsletters in my inbox."*

> [!WARNING]
> **⚠️ Browser agents need supervision**
> An AI that can click around websites while you're logged in is powerful, and web pages can contain hidden
> instructions trying to trick it. Watch it work, approve anything important, and keep it away from banking. See
> [Computer Use & Browser Agents](../part-7-building-with-ai/71-computer-use-and-browser-agents.md).

## 🔌 Connectors, files and more

<details class="eli5">

You can connect Perplexity to your email, calendar and other apps, upload your own files, and use it for shopping and

## 🍳 Step-by-step recipes

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Four real jobs you can do with Perplexity today.

</details>

**Recipe 1: Buy the right thing**

1. Pro Search → *"Best cordless vacuum under $300 for a small apartment with a cat. Compare the top 4 in a table:
   battery life, weight, noise, reviews. Cite recent reviews."*
2. Open two review sources before buying.

**Recipe 2: A research report you can trust**

1. Research mode → *"What does the evidence say about intermittent fasting for people over 50? Summarize the strongest
   studies and their limitations."*
2. Take the report (and your questions) to your doctor.

**Recipe 3: Plan a move with a Space**

1. Create a Space "📦 Moving to Denver" with your budget and priorities.
2. Ask about neighborhoods, schools, commute times and costs. Everything stays in one place.

**Recipe 4: Fact-check a viral claim**

1. *"Is it true that [claim]? Check fact-checkers and primary sources, and tell me what's confirmed and what isn't."*

## 💡 Pro tips and limitations

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Clever tricks Perplexity fans know, and the things to watch out for.

</details>

**Pro tips**

- 🎯 **Ask comparison questions in tables:** Perplexity is excellent at structured, sourced comparisons.
- 🧑‍🎓 **Academic focus** for studies and papers.
- 🔁 **Rewrite with a different model** (Pro) to compare how models interpret the same sources.
- 🗞️ **Follow topics** in Discover for a personalized news feed.

**Limitations**

- It can **misread or over-trust** a weak source. Click through for anything important.
- **Less suited** to long creative writing or personal chit-chat than ChatGPT or Claude.
- Agentic browsing is still **early**. Supervise it.

## 🎯 Key takeaways

- Perplexity is an **answer engine**: written answers with **numbered, clickable sources**.
- Use **Search** for quick facts, **Pro Search** for harder questions, **Research** for reports and **Labs** for
  finished outputs.
- **Spaces** organize research with instructions and files; **Comet** puts an AI assistant in your browser.
- Always **click the sources** behind important claims.
