# 28 · Apple Intelligence, Siri, Alexa+ & Built-In Assistants 🍎

> ⏱️ 7 min read · 🎯 iPhone, Echo, Galaxy and Pixel owners · 🧰 Needs: the devices you already own

**The assistants built into your devices have had a glow-up.** Siri was rebuilt with Apple Intelligence and Google's
Gemini models, Alexa+ turned Amazon's speakers into real conversationalists, Gemini replaced Google Assistant on Android
and Nest, and Samsung's Galaxy AI translates phone calls live. This chapter goes deeper than the [quick
tour](../part-1-ai-from-zero/12-ai-everywhere.md): setup, the best things to ask, privacy, and how these hands-free
helpers team up with chatbot apps.

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

The assistants built into phones, speakers, TVs and cars have improved dramatically with modern AI. They're best for quick, hands-free tasks such as timers, messages, translation and smart home control.

- **Apple:** Apple Intelligence and a rebuilt Siri, with a strong privacy design.
- **Amazon and Google:** Alexa+ on Echo devices; Gemini on Android, Nest speakers and Google TV.
- **Samsung:** Galaxy AI features like live call translation, alongside Gemini.
- **Know when to switch:** for long conversations or complex projects, a full chatbot app works better.

</details>

## 🍎 Apple Intelligence and the new Siri

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Apple Intelligence powers a rebuilt Siri (iOS 27, September 2026) that understands what's on your screen, draws on your messages and calendar privately, and takes actions across apps. Larger requests use Apple's Private Cloud Compute, and Siri can hand questions to ChatGPT or Gemini with your permission.

</details>

With **iOS 27** (September 2026), Apple shipped a rebuilt Siri. Apple's own models run on-device, and bigger requests go
to Apple's **Private Cloud Compute**, which uses a custom Google Gemini-based model. The new Siri:

- 💬 **Converses naturally:** follow-ups, corrections mid-sentence, a chat-style interface you can also type to.
- 👀 **Understands your screen:** *"Add this address to Mum's contact,"* *"Summarize this article."*
- 🧠 **Knows your personal context** (privately): *"When is Jamie's recital?"* (from Messages or Mail), *"Show me the
  photos from the beach day with Priya."*
- ⚙️ **Acts across apps:** *"Send the pictures from yesterday to Dad and tell him we'll call Sunday."*
- 🔎 **"Search or Ask":** a system-wide gesture to search your device and ask Siri anything.

**Setup:** Settings → **Apple Intelligence & Siri**. Requires a recent device (Apple Intelligence runs on newer iPhones,
iPads and Macs) and a supported language and region.

**Other Apple Intelligence tools:** Writing Tools (rewrite, proofread, summarize anywhere), Visual Intelligence (camera
look-ups), Clean Up in Photos, Image Playground and Genmoji, notification summaries, and Live Translation in Messages,
FaceTime and phone calls.

> [!TIP]
> **💡 Siri + your favorite chatbot**
> Siri can hand bigger questions to other assistant apps you install (ChatGPT has long been built in as an option).
> And every chatbot app works with the **Action Button**, Lock Screen widgets and **Shortcuts**, so you can launch
> ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude voice mode with a single press.

## 🔒 Apple's privacy approach

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Apple processes as many requests as possible on your device. Larger requests go to Private Cloud Compute, servers designed so your data isn't stored or accessible to Apple, and independent researchers can inspect the system.

</details>

- **On-device first:** many requests never leave your device.
- **Private Cloud Compute:** larger requests go to Apple-run servers designed so your data isn't stored or accessible to
  Apple, and independent researchers can inspect the system. Apple says this applies to the Gemini-based model too.
- **Hand-offs to other apps** (like ChatGPT) follow that app's privacy policy, and Siri asks before sending.

## 🔊 Alexa+ (Amazon)

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Alexa+ is Amazon's rebuilt, generative-AI Alexa. It understands natural requests, remembers preferences, and can plan, order groceries, make bookings and control smart home devices. It's included with Prime in the US or available as a standalone subscription.

</details>

**Alexa+** is Amazon's rebuilt, generative-AI Alexa, available to everyone in the US (included with **Prime**, or as a
standalone subscription), on Echo devices, Fire TV, Fire tablets, the Alexa app and **Alexa.com**. It's powered by
Amazon's Nova models and models from Anthropic.

| Ask it… | Example |
|---|---|
| 🏠 Smart home in plain English | *"It's too bright and a bit chilly in here."* |
| 🛒 Shopping and groceries | *"Add everything for tacos on Friday to my grocery cart."* |
| 📅 Family organizing | *"Remember that Leo has swimming every Tuesday at 5,"* then *"What's on for the kids this week?"* |
| 🍽️ Bookings | *"Book a table for four somewhere Italian nearby on Saturday."* |
| 📄 Documents | Share a school newsletter or recipe (via email or the app) and ask about it |
| 🍳 Kitchen help | Timers, conversions, substitutions, step-by-step recipes |

Non-Prime users can also try a free text chat at **Alexa.com** and in the Alexa app.

**Privacy:** review and delete voice recordings in the Alexa app (**Settings → Alexa Privacy**); mute the microphone
button when you want quiet.

## 🤖 Google: Gemini on Android and at home

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Gemini has replaced Google Assistant on Android phones and on Google's Nest speakers and displays, bringing more natural conversation, screen awareness and smarter home control.

</details>

- **Android phones:** Gemini is the default assistant (power button or *"Hey Google"*), with Gemini Live, "Ask about
  screen," Circle to Search and app actions. Full guide: [Gemini](19-gemini.md).
- **Google Home / Nest:** **Gemini for Home** is replacing Google Assistant on Nest speakers and displays: more natural
  conversation, better smart home control (*"turn off everything downstairs except the porch light"*), and smarter
  camera notifications on supported plans.
- **Pixel phones** add extras such as Magic Editor, Call Screen, Best Take and on-device AI features.

## 📱 Samsung Galaxy AI

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Samsung Galaxy phones combine Galaxy AI features, such as real-time call translation, note formatting and photo editing, with Gemini as the main assistant. The table describes each feature.

</details>

Samsung Galaxy phones combine **Galaxy AI** features with Gemini:

| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| 📞 **Live Translate** | Translates phone calls in real time, both ways |
| 🗣️ **Interpreter** | Split-screen, face-to-face conversation translation |
| 📝 **Note Assist** | Summarizes and formats notes, translates them |
| 🌐 **Browsing Assist** | Summarizes web pages |
| 🖼️ **Photo Assist** | Moves, removes or resizes objects in photos |
| 🔍 **Circle to Search** | Search anything on screen |
| 📰 **Now Brief** | A personalized daily summary |

Find them in **Settings → Galaxy AI**. Some features process on-device (you can choose "process data only on device").

## 📺 TVs, watches and cars

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

AI assistants are also built into TVs (for finding shows), smartwatches (for quick questions and replies) and cars (for hands-free messages, navigation and questions while driving).

</details>

- **TVs:** Gemini on Google TV, Copilot on some Samsung and LG TVs, Alexa+ on Fire TV. *"Find a funny movie we'll all
  like, under two hours, that we haven't watched."*
- **Watches:** Siri on Apple Watch, Gemini on Wear OS and Galaxy Watch. Quick questions, reminders, messages.
- **Cars:** Siri (CarPlay), Gemini (Android Auto), Grok (Teslas), and brand assistants built on big AI models.

## 🧭 Which built-in helper for which job?

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Built-in assistants are best for quick, hands-free tasks and anything involving your device's own apps. For longer conversations, research or creative projects, use a full chatbot app. The table matches common jobs to the best tool.

</details>

| Job | Best tool |
|---|---|
| Timers, alarms, lights, music, quick facts | Your **built-in** assistant (Siri, Alexa+, Gemini) |
| Anything about your phone's own apps and data | The **phone's** assistant (Siri on iPhone, Gemini on Android) |
| Live call and face-to-face translation | **Galaxy AI**, Apple's Live Translation, Pixel features |
| Household shopping and routines | **Alexa+** or **Gemini for Home** |
| Long conversations, writing, research, projects | A **chatbot app** (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude…) |

The winning combo for most people: **the built-in assistant for hands-free moments, plus one chatbot app for everything
bigger.**

## 🎯 Key takeaways

- **Siri** was rebuilt in **iOS 27** with Apple Intelligence, on-screen awareness, personal context and app actions,
  using Gemini-based models through Private Cloud Compute.
- **Alexa+** is conversational and agentic, free with **Prime** in the US.
- **Gemini** replaced Google Assistant on **Android** and is replacing it on **Nest** devices.
- **Galaxy AI** adds live call translation, note tools and photo edits.
- Use built-in helpers for **hands-free quick jobs**, a chatbot app for **deeper work**.
