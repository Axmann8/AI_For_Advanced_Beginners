## 🔐 Privacy at a glance

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Different AI companies handle your chats differently. Some use them to train their AI unless you switch it off, and some
keep data in other countries. Check the switches.

</details>

| Assistant | Training on your chats (consumer plans) | Worth knowing |
|---|---|---|
| 💬 ChatGPT | On by default; opt out in Data controls | Temporary chats aren't used for training; Health data is kept separate |
| ✨ Gemini | Tied to "Gemini Apps Activity"; can turn off | Human reviewers may see some chats; don't share confidential info |
| 🧡 Claude | You choose in Privacy settings | Incognito chats aren't used for training |
| 🪟 Copilot | Adjustable in privacy settings | Work accounts get enterprise data protection |
| ⚡ Grok | Adjustable in Data controls (and X settings) | Past controversies over image editing of real people |
| 🔎 Perplexity | Adjustable in settings | Comet browser can see the pages you browse |
| 👓 Meta AI | Used to personalize, including ads | Posts to the Discover feed can be public; check before sharing |
| 🐋 DeepSeek | Yes | **Data stored in China**; restricted on government devices in several countries |
| 🌬️ Le Chat | Opt-out available | European company, subject to EU rules |

**Business and enterprise plans** of the major assistants generally don't train on your data by default. For the full
picture, see [Privacy & Your Data](../part-12-mastery/104-privacy-and-your-data.md).

## 🌍 Where you can use them

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Most AIs work in most countries and speak lots of languages, but some features only arrive in some places first.

</details>

- **Countries:** ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, Perplexity and Le Chat are available in most countries. Some are
  restricted in certain regions (for example, several Western assistants aren't officially available in China, and
  some features launch in the US first or arrive later in the EU).
- **Languages:** all the big assistants handle dozens of languages. Qwen (119 languages) and Gemini are especially
  broad. Quality is usually best in English and major world languages.
- **Ages:** usually 13+ with parental permission or 18+. Check each service's rules, and see [Parents, Teachers &
  Students](../part-11-ai-for-life-and-work/98-parents-teachers-and-students.md).

## 🎯 Key takeaways

- There are **nine major assistants** plus regional options, built-in assistants and hubs, and all are good for everyday
  tasks.
- They differ in **ecosystem** (Google, Microsoft, Meta, Apple), **special powers** (sources, video, glasses, coding)
  and **personality**.
- **Every one has a free tier**; paid tiers add limits and power.
- **Privacy defaults differ**: check training settings, and be careful with services that store data abroad.
- Each has a **full guide** in this part. Bookmark yours!
