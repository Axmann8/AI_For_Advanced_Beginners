# 26 · Mistral Le Chat: The Complete Guide 🌬️

> ⏱️ 5 min read · 🎯 Beginners, Europeans and privacy-minded users · 🧰 Needs: a free Le Chat account (chat.mistral.ai)

**Le Chat is the assistant from Mistral AI, France's AI champion and Europe's leading AI lab.** It's fast (very fast),
capable, generous on its free tier, and comes with memories, connectors, deep research, image creation and a
friendly price for Pro. For anyone who wants a strong alternative to the American giants, or prefers a company under
European privacy rules, Le Chat is well worth a look. 🇪🇺

<details class="eli5" open>
<summary>🧸 ELI5: This chapter in 30 seconds</summary>

Le Chat ("the cat" in French 🐈) is an AI helper made by a company in France. It answers super quickly, remembers things
about you if you want, can look things up, make pictures, and connect to your apps. Because it's European, it follows
Europe's strict privacy rules.

</details>

## 🚪 Getting started

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Sign up on the website or app, switch on memories if you like, and start chatting.

</details>

3. Turn on **Memories** if you want personalized answers (you can even **import your ChatGPT memories** in one click).
4. Browse **Connectors** to link the apps you use.

## 🧠 Memories and connectors

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Memories let Le Chat remember you. Connectors let it reach your other apps. You decide what it keeps and what it can
touch.

</details>

- **Memories:** Le Chat saves useful facts and preferences (you'll see when it does) and uses them to personalize
  answers. You can view, edit and delete every memory, and **import your ChatGPT memories** if you're switching.
- **Connectors:** built on **MCP**, the open standard ([MCP Explained](../part-4-mcp-and-connectors/38-mcp-explained.md)).
  Search, summarize and act across tools like GitHub, Asana and Outlook, or add your own custom MCP connector. Approve
  actions before they happen.

## 🔐 Privacy and settings

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Le Chat follows Europe's privacy rules. You can stop your chats being used for training, and delete memories and
history.

</details>

- **European company, EU rules:** Mistral is subject to the GDPR and the EU AI Act.
- **Training opt-out:** turn off use of your conversations for training in settings (business plans don't train on your
  data by default).
- **Memories:** fully viewable and deletable; turn off entirely if you prefer.
- **Enterprise self-hosting:** organizations can run Le Chat on their own infrastructure.

## 💡 Pro tips and limitations

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Clever tricks Le Chat fans know, and the things to watch out for.

</details>

**Pro tips**

- ⚡ **Speed for drafting:** fast answers make rapid back-and-forth editing a joy.
- 🌍 **Multilingual reasoning:** great for French, German, Spanish, Italian and more.
- 🧑‍💻 **For builders:** Mistral's open-weight models and its coding agent (**Mistral Vibe**) are popular with
  developers.

**Limitations**

- Fewer integrations than **Google or Microsoft** ecosystems.
- Voice, video and agent features are **less mature** than the biggest rivals.

- A great **main assistant or second opinion**, especially for Europeans and privacy-minded users.
