# 19 · Google Gemini: The Complete Guide ✨

> ⏱️ 12 min read · 🎯 Beginners to advanced beginners · 🧰 Needs: a Google account (gemini.google.com or the Gemini app)

**Gemini is Google's AI assistant, and its superpower is Google itself.** It's built into Android phones, Chrome, Gmail,
Docs, Google Home and more, and (if you let it) it can use your own Gmail, Calendar, Photos and Drive to give answers
that are truly personal. Add excellent voice-and-camera conversations, image and video creation, Deep Research and
custom Gems, and you've got one of the most capable assistants around. Here's how to get the most from it.

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Gemini is Google's AI assistant. Its biggest advantage is integration with Google's services: with your permission, it can use Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Photos, Maps and YouTube to give answers specific to your life.

- **Core features:** conversation, Gemini Live voice and camera, image and video creation, and web search.
- **Personal features:** Personal Intelligence, Gems (custom assistants) and Gemini inside Gmail, Docs and Chrome.
- **Advanced tools:** Deep Research, Canvas, scheduled actions, Gemini Notebook and developer tools.
- **Privacy:** how to control activity history and app access.

</details>

## 🚪 Getting started

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Getting started with Gemini is quick if you already have a Google account.

1. Open Gemini: long-press the power button on Android, install the app on iPhone, or visit gemini.google.com.
2. Sign in with your Google account.
3. Choose which Google apps Gemini may access, then start asking.

</details>

1. **Open Gemini:** on Android, long-press the power button or say *"Hey Google"*; on iPhone, install **Google Gemini**;
   on a computer, go to **gemini.google.com**.
2. **Sign in** with your Google account.
3. **Tell it about you:** Settings → **Personal context / Saved info** → add preferences (*"I'm vegetarian," "I live in
   Toronto," "Keep answers short"*).
4. **Choose connected apps:** Settings → **Apps** (or the Tools menu) → pick which Google apps Gemini can use.
5. **Pick a model:** the model picker at the top offers faster and more thoughtful modes. Leave it on the default to
   start.

## 💳 Plans in plain English

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Gemini's free tier is generous. Google AI plans (Plus, Pro and Ultra) add the most capable models, more image and video generation, and extra cloud storage. The table summarizes each plan.

</details>

| Plan | What you get (roughly) |
|---|---|
| **Free** | Gemini chat, Live voice, image creation, Gems, some Deep Research, Personal Intelligence (where available) |
| **Google AI Plus** (some countries) | More of the good stuff at a lower price, plus storage |

Plans are sold through **Google One** and often include cloud storage for the whole family. **Students** often get
discounted or free offers, and some phones (like new Pixels) come with trial months.

## 👤 Personal Intelligence: Gemini that knows your stuff

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Personal Intelligence is an opt-in feature that lets Gemini draw on your Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Photos, YouTube and Maps to answer questions about your own life.

1. Open **Settings** and turn on **Personal Intelligence** (or connect individual apps).
2. Ask questions that need your data, such as *"When is my next dentist appointment?"*
3. Review or turn off access at any time in the same settings.

</details>

This is Gemini's headline feature. **Personal Intelligence** (opt-in) lets Gemini draw on your Google apps (Gmail,
Calendar, Drive, Photos, YouTube, Maps, Search) and your past chats, so you can ask things only *you* could answer
before:

- *"When does my flight to Rome land, and how do I get from the airport to my hotel?"* (from your booking email + Maps)
- *"What was the name of that pasta recipe video I watched last week?"* (YouTube history)
- *"Find the photo of our car's license plate."* (Google Photos)
- *"What have I promised people this week?"* (Gmail + Calendar)
- *"Draft a reply to Jamie's email about the school fundraiser."*

**Control:** you choose which apps are connected (Settings → Apps / Personal Intelligence), and you can switch it off
for a single prompt from the **Tools** menu. It's available in many countries worldwide, though not yet in Europe.

> [!TIP]
> **💡 The Daily Brief**
> Gemini can put together a **Daily Brief**: a short morning overview of your priorities from Gmail, Calendar and your
> chats. Ask *"Give me my daily brief"* or look for it on the Gemini home screen.

## 🎙️ Gemini Live: talk, show and share

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Gemini Live is a real-time voice conversation. During a session, you can turn on your camera or share your screen so Gemini can see what you're asking about and guide you.

</details>

Tap **Live** (the waveform icon) to start a flowing voice conversation. Then:

- 📹 **Camera:** point at the back of your TV (*"which HDMI port?"*), your garden (*"why are these leaves yellow?"*),
  your fridge (*"what can I cook?"*).
- 📱 **Screen sharing:** *"Help me compare these two phone plans I'm looking at."*
- ✅ **Actions:** Live can work with your apps: add calendar events, create reminders, draft emails, get directions.
- 🗣️ **Practice:** interview rehearsal, language conversation, talking through a decision.

## 💎 Gems: your custom assistants

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Gems are custom versions of Gemini with saved instructions and optional files, ideal for tasks you repeat.

1. In the sidebar, open **Gems** and choose **New Gem**.
2. Name it and write instructions describing its job and your preferences.
3. Add reference files if useful, save, and start chatting with it.

</details>

**Gems** are custom versions of Gemini with saved instructions (and optional files):

1. Sidebar → **Gems** → **New Gem**.
2. Name it (*"🥗 Meal Planner"*) and write instructions: who you are, what it should do, the format you want.
3. Optionally add **knowledge files** (your family's favorite recipes, a style guide).
4. Save, and chat with it any time from the sidebar.

Google also includes **premade Gems** (a learning coach, a brainstormer, a career guide, a writing editor and more) to
try or copy.

## 🔬 Deep Research and Canvas

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

**Deep Research** reads many websites (and, optionally, your Gmail and Drive) and produces a detailed, cited report. **Canvas** is a shared workspace where Gemini creates documents, quizzes and small interactive apps with you.

</details>

- **Deep Research:** choose it from the Tools menu, ask a big question, approve the research plan, and in a few minutes
  get a detailed, cited report. It can include your own **Gmail, Drive and Chat** content if you allow it (*"Research
  the best project-management tools for us, considering the requirements in my team's emails"*). You can turn a report
  into an **Audio Overview** (a podcast-style summary), a quiz or an infographic.
- **Canvas:** choose Canvas from Tools to co-write documents, generate **web pages, quizzes, infographics and simple
  apps**, and edit them with Gemini side by side. Export docs to Google Docs.

## 🎨 Images and video

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Gemini creates and edits images from your descriptions and is especially good at editing your own photos while keeping people recognizable. Paid plans add short video generation with sound.

</details>

- **Images:** Google's image models (nicknamed **Nano Banana**) are excellent at **editing your own photos** while
  keeping people and pets looking like themselves: *"Put me and my sister on a beach at sunset,"* *"Change the sofa to
  green velvet,"* *"Restore and colorize this old family photo."*
- **Video:** **Veo** creates short, realistic video clips **with sound** from a description or a photo (paid plans get
  more): *"A golden retriever surfing a small wave, slow motion, sunny day."*
- Newer creative tools (rolling out) add video editing and custom AI avatars.

All Google AI images and videos carry an invisible **SynthID** watermark, and Gemini can check whether an image was made
with Google AI.

## 📧 Gemini in Gmail, Docs, Chrome and more

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Gemini is built into Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Meet, Chrome and other Google apps, so you can draft, summarize and analyze without leaving the app you're using. The table shows what it does in each.

</details>

| Where | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Gmail** | "Help me write," summarize long threads, find info across your inbox |
| **Docs** | Draft, rewrite, summarize; "help me create" a document from notes |
| **Sheets** | Build tables, formulas and charts from plain-English requests |
| **Slides** | Generate slides and images |
| **Drive** | Ask questions across your files |
| **Meet** | Take notes and summarize meetings (on supported plans) |
| **Chrome** | **Gemini in Chrome**: ask about the page, compare across tabs, and (rolling out) let it do browser tasks |
| **Google Home** | Gemini for Home on Nest speakers and displays: natural conversation and smart home control |
| **Android Auto** | Hands-free messages, directions and questions while driving |

Availability depends on your plan (personal Google AI plans or business Google Workspace) and country. More in
[Google Workspace & Microsoft 365 AI](../part-6-ai-in-your-apps/55-google-and-microsoft-ai.md).

## 🤖 Agents and scheduled actions

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Scheduled actions let Gemini run a task on a regular schedule, such as a morning summary of your calendar. Google's agent features can also carry out multi-step tasks on the web for you.

</details>

- **Scheduled actions:** *"Every morning at 7, summarize my calendar and any urgent emails,"* *"Every Friday, send me
  three weekend ideas near me."*
- **Agent features:** Google's newest agent features (including **Spark**, which works on tasks across your digital life,
  and background **information agents** that monitor topics and send you updates) are rolling out, first to Google AI
  Pro and Ultra subscribers in the US.
- **Gemini in Chrome** is gaining the ability to carry out web tasks in your browser (with your approval).

## 📓 Gemini Notebook and learning tools

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM) answers questions based only on the sources you upload and can generate study guides, flashcards and Audio Overviews. Gemini also offers guided learning modes for studying.

</details>

- **Gemini Notebook** (formerly **NotebookLM**): upload sources (PDFs, websites, YouTube videos, notes) and get
  grounded answers, study guides, mind maps, flashcards and **Audio Overviews** (two AI hosts discussing your material).
  Full guide: [Gemini Notebook Masterclass](../part-8-knowledge-and-memory/76-notebooklm-masterclass.md).
- **Guided Learning:** a tutor mode that teaches step by step and checks your understanding.
- **Student hub:** organize classes and personalized study notebooks (on web and mobile).

## 🍳 Step-by-step recipes

These four recipes walk through real tasks in Gemini step by step, combining Personal Intelligence, Gems, Deep Research and Live.

## 🔐 Privacy and settings

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

The table lists Gemini's main privacy settings, including activity history, auto-delete and app access, and explains what each one controls.

</details>

| Setting | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Gemini Apps Activity** (Keep Activity) | Settings → Activity | Controls whether chats are saved and used to improve Google AI; set auto-delete (for example 3, 18 or 36 months) |
| **Connected apps** | Settings → Apps | Choose which Google apps Gemini can use |
| **Personal Intelligence** | Settings, or per-prompt in Tools | Turn it off entirely or for one question |
| **Temporary chat** | Chat menu | A chat that isn't saved to your activity or used for personalization |
| **Saved info** | Settings → Personal context | Review what Gemini knows about you |
| **Public links** | Settings → Your public links | Remove shared chats |

> [!NOTE]
> **📌 Human review**
> Google says a sample of conversations may be reviewed by trained reviewers to improve its products (disconnected from
> your account). Don't enter confidential information you wouldn't want a reviewer to see, or turn off activity saving.
> Work and school Google Workspace accounts have stronger protections.

## 💡 Pro tips and limitations

<details class="keypoints" open>
<summary>✅ Key Points & Steps</summary>

These tips help you get more out of Gemini, followed by its current limitations so you know when to double-check its answers.

</details>

**Pro tips**

- 🔍 **Double-check button:** use Gemini's "double-check response" option to highlight claims Google Search supports
  or contradicts.
- 🗺️ **Ask with Maps:** Gemini gives rich answers with real place details, ratings and directions.
- ▶️ **YouTube summaries:** paste a video link and ask for a summary or the key steps.
- 📸 **Is it AI?** Upload an image and ask if it was made with Google AI (SynthID check).
- 🧩 **Mix apps in one request:** *"Find the address in my email and give me directions avoiding tolls."*

**Limitations**

- Features and availability **vary a lot by country, age and plan**.
- Personal answers are only as good as your **connected data** (and it can misread emails).
- Like every assistant, it can be **confidently wrong**. Check important facts.

## 🎯 Key takeaways

- Gemini's superpower is **Google integration**: Gmail, Calendar, Photos, Maps, YouTube, Chrome, Android, Home.
- **Personal Intelligence** (opt-in) answers questions about *your* life.
- **Gemini Live** adds voice, camera and screen sharing; **Gems** are your custom assistants.
- **Deep Research, Canvas, Nano Banana images and Veo video** are standout tools.
- Manage privacy with **Gemini Apps Activity**, connected apps and temporary chats.
