# 30 · Same Task, Every Assistant: Head-to-Head Showdowns 🥊

> ⏱️ 7 min read (plus an evening of fun testing) · 🎯 Anyone choosing or combining assistants · 🧰 Needs: free accounts on 2–4 assistants

**Which AI is best? The honest answer is "best at *what*, for *whom*?"** This chapter runs ten everyday challenges
across the major assistants and explains which ones tend to shine at each task and *why* (usually because of a specific
feature). Then it hands you a scorecard to run your own showdown, because your taste and your tasks matter more than any
ranking. And rankings change every few months anyway! 🏁

<details class="eli5" open>
<summary>🧸 ELI5: This chapter in 30 seconds</summary>

We give the same jobs to lots of AIs, like a cooking competition where every chef makes the same dish. Some are better at
pictures, some at research, some at writing. The winner depends on the job, so it's smart to know which AI to ask for
which job.

</details>

<!-- in-this-chapter -->

> [!NOTE]
> **📌 How to read these showdowns**
> AI models improve constantly, and quality differences between the top assistants are often small. So instead of
> declaring permanent winners, each showdown tells you **what to look for** and **which features give certain
> assistants an edge** for that kind of task. Run the prompts yourself. It's the only ranking that counts.

## ✉️ Showdown 1: The tricky email

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Every AI can write a good email. The differences are in how natural it sounds and whether the AI lives inside your email
app.

</details>

> *"Write a short email to my landlord. The heating has been broken for 5 days, I've reported it twice with no
> response, and I have a baby at home. Firm but polite, and mention what I'll do if it isn't fixed by Friday."*

**Look for:** natural tone (not robotic), the right firmness, no invented facts, a sensible next step.

**Who tends to shine:** all the major assistants do this well. **Claude** and **ChatGPT** are often praised for natural,
human-sounding prose; **Gemini in Gmail** and **Copilot in Outlook** win on convenience because they draft right where
you send.

## 🛒 Showdown 2: Research a purchase

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

For shopping research, the best AIs show where their information came from, so you can check the reviews yourself.

</details>

> *"Compare the 4 best air purifiers under $250 for a bedroom with a dog. Table: room size, filter cost per year, noise,
> and what reviewers complain about. Cite recent reviews."*

**Look for:** real, current products; working source links; honest downsides; filter costs (often forgotten).

**Who tends to shine:** **Perplexity** (sources are its whole identity), and the **deep research** modes in ChatGPT,
Gemini and Claude for more thorough reports. Always click two sources before buying.

## 📰 Showdown 3: What's happening right now

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

For today's news, you need an AI that searches the internet and tells you where it read things.

</details>

> *"What are the three biggest news stories in [your country] today? For each: what happened, why it matters, and
> links to two reputable sources."*

**Look for:** today's date on sources, reputable outlets, a clear line between confirmed facts and speculation.

**Who tends to shine:** **Perplexity** for sourced summaries, **Gemini** and **ChatGPT** search, and **Grok** for what
people on X are saying (just remember that chatter isn't confirmation).

## 📄 Showdown 4: Understand a long document

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Give each AI the same long document and see which one understands it best and points to the right pages.

</details>

> Upload a 30+ page PDF (a lease, a policy, a report): *"Summarize the key points, list anything that could cost me
> money or catch me out, and quote the relevant sections with page numbers."*

**Look for:** accurate quotes (check them!), correct page numbers, nothing important missed.

**Who tends to shine:** **Claude** and **Gemini** are known for handling long documents well; **Kimi** is built for very
long inputs; **Gemini Notebook** is superb when you want answers grounded strictly in your sources.

## 🖼️ Showdown 5: Make an image

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Ask each AI for the same picture and see whose looks best, especially if the picture has words in it.

</details>

> *"A poster for a neighborhood book swap: 'BOOK SWAP · Saturday 10am · Oak Street Library'. Cozy illustration of
> stacked books and a cat, warm autumn colors."*

**Look for:** correctly spelled text, following every instruction, a style you like.

**Who tends to shine:** **ChatGPT** is strong at text inside images and following detailed layouts; **Gemini** (Nano
Banana) excels at editing your own photos consistently; **Grok Imagine** and **Meta AI** are quick and fun. (Claude
doesn't make photos.)

## 🎬 Showdown 6: Make a short video

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Only some AIs can make videos. Ask them for the same little clip and compare.

</details>

> *"A 6-second video of a hot air balloon rising over misty hills at sunrise, gentle wind sounds."*

**Look for:** realistic motion, matching sound, no weird glitches.

**Who tends to shine:** **Gemini** (Veo, with sound), **Grok Imagine** and **Meta AI** create video in-app; specialist
tools like Runway and Kling go further ([Video & Audio Production](../part-10-creative-ai/85-video-and-audio-production.md)).

## 🗓️ Showdown 7: Plan from *your* data

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

The best AI for your own life is the one connected to your email and calendar.

</details>

> *"Look at my calendar and email for next week. What's coming up, what do I need to prepare, and is anything
> clashing?"*

**Look for:** accuracy about your real commitments, and nothing invented.

**Who tends to shine:** **Gemini** (Personal Intelligence across Gmail and Calendar) if you live in Google; **Copilot**
with Outlook in Microsoft 365; **ChatGPT**, **Claude** and **Le Chat** through connectors. The winner is whichever
connects to where your life actually lives.

## 🧮 Showdown 8: A genuinely tricky problem

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

For hard puzzles and math, switch on each AI's "thinking" mode and see who gets it right.

</details>

> *"We're 5 adults splitting a 4-night holiday cottage costing £1,340. Two people stay only 3 nights, and one person
> paid the £200 deposit. Work out exactly what each person owes whom, showing your working."*

**Look for:** correct math, clear working, stated assumptions.

**Who tends to shine:** use the **thinking/reasoning mode** in any assistant: ChatGPT **Thinking**, Gemini's thinking
modes (and **Deep Think**), Claude **extended thinking**, Grok **Expert**, DeepSeek **DeepThink**. Most get this right
in reasoning mode; fast modes are where slips happen. Check the numbers yourself.

## 🎙️ Showdown 9: A voice conversation

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Talk to each AI out loud and see which one feels most like a natural conversation.

</details>

> In voice mode: *"I'm going for a walk. Teach me something fascinating about the ocean, and let me interrupt with
> questions."*

**Look for:** natural voice, handles interruptions, keeps the thread, feels like a conversation.

**Who tends to shine:** **ChatGPT** voice and **Gemini Live** (both with camera sharing) are highly polished; **Meta
AI** on glasses is the most hands-free; **Grok** offers fun personalities; **Claude** voice is great for thinking out
loud.

## 🛠️ Showdown 10: Build a mini-app

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Ask each AI to build the same little app and see whose works best and looks nicest.

</details>

> *"Build a simple interactive tool where I enter my household chores and family members, and it creates a fair weekly
> rota I can print. Cheerful design."*

**Look for:** does it actually work? Is it pleasant to use? Can you share it?

**Who tends to shine:** **Claude Artifacts** (interactive apps you can share by link), **ChatGPT** (Canvas and its
site-building tools), **Gemini Canvas** and Google **AI Studio**. For bigger apps, see [Vibe
Coding](../part-7-building-with-ai/65-vibe-coding-your-first-app.md).

## 📋 Run your own showdown

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Pick your own jobs, give them to a few AIs, and score them. Your own test is the one that counts.

## 🎯 Key takeaways

- There's **no single best assistant**; there's a best assistant **for each task and person**.
- Edges usually come from **features**: sources (Perplexity), integration (Gemini, Copilot), long documents (Claude,
  Gemini, Kimi), images and video (ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Meta), artifacts (Claude).
- Use **reasoning modes** for hard problems in any assistant.
- **Run your own showdown** with your real tasks and repeat it every few months.
