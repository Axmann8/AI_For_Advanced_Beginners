# 18 · ChatGPT: The Complete Guide 💬

> ⏱️ 12 min read · 🎯 Beginners to advanced beginners · 🧰 Needs: a free ChatGPT account (chatgpt.com)

**ChatGPT is the assistant that started the AI boom in late 2022, and it's still the most-used AI app in the world.**
It's a genuine all-rounder: conversation, voice, vision, images, web search, deep research, memory, projects, apps and
an agent that can do multi-step work for you. This guide takes you from your first chat to the power features, with
step-by-step recipes and the privacy switches worth knowing.

<details class="eli5" open>
<summary>🧸 ELI5: This chapter in 30 seconds</summary>

ChatGPT is like a Swiss Army knife of AI: it can chat, talk out loud, look at photos, draw pictures, search the internet,
write long reports, remember things about you, and even do jobs on the web for you. This chapter shows you where each
tool is and how to use it.

</details>

## 🚪 Getting started

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Sign up with your email or Google account, tell ChatGPT a bit about yourself, pick a personality style you like, and
start chatting.

</details>

1. **Sign up** at **chatgpt.com** or in the ChatGPT app (maker: **OpenAI**). Use email or "Continue with Google / Apple
   / Microsoft." You can chat without an account, but an account saves your history and unlocks features.
2. **Personalize:** profile picture → **Settings → Personalization**:
   - **Custom instructions:** what ChatGPT should know about you and how to respond (template in
     [Getting Set Up](../part-1-ai-from-zero/05-getting-set-up.md#-tell-it-about-you-it-makes-a-huge-difference)).
   - **Personality / style:** choose a default style (for example more concise, more friendly, more professional).
   - **Memory:** on or off (more below).
3. **Install the apps** you'll use: phone app for voice and camera; the **desktop app** for work.
4. **Security:** Settings → **Security** → turn on multi-factor authentication.

## 💳 Plans in plain English

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Free is good for most people. Paid plans give you more messages, the smartest models and extra tools.

</details>

| Plan | Who it's for | What you get (roughly) |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | Everyone starting out | Fast model, limited Thinking, search, voice, some image creation, projects and memory |
| **Go** | Budget-conscious regular users | Higher limits than Free at a low price (available in many countries) |
| **Plus** | Daily users | Much higher limits, full Thinking model, more images, deep research, agent features |
| **Pro** (more than one level) | Heavy/professional users | Very high limits and access to the most powerful **Pro** models |
| **Business / Enterprise / Edu** | Organizations | Admin controls, no training on your data by default, shared workspaces |

Check **chatgpt.com/pricing** for current prices in your country.

## 🧠 Models and modes

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

ChatGPT has a fast brain for quick questions and a thinking brain for hard problems. Usually it picks for you, but you
can choose.

</details>

At the top of a chat you'll see a **model picker**. The names change over time, but the idea stays the same:

| Mode | Use it for |
|---|---|
| ⚡ **Auto** (default) | Let ChatGPT decide when to think harder. Great for most people |
| 🏃 **Instant / fast** | Quick answers, casual chat, simple writing |
| 🤔 **Thinking** | Math, planning, tricky comparisons, analysis, coding. It takes longer and is more careful |
| 🧠 **Pro** (top plans) | The hardest problems, where accuracy matters most |

**Tip:** if an answer to a hard question seems shallow, switch to **Thinking** and ask again, or just say *"think
harder about this."*

## 🗂️ Projects: your organized workspaces

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Projects are like folders for your chats. Put a topic's chats and files in one place, add special instructions, and
ChatGPT remembers the context every time you work on it.

</details>

**Projects** group related chats with **shared files and instructions**, so ChatGPT always has the right context:

1. Sidebar → **New project** → give it a name and emoji (for example "🏡 Kitchen renovation").
2. Open the project's settings → add **instructions** (*"We're renovating a small kitchen on a $15k budget. Keep advice
   practical and UK-specific."*).
3. **Add files:** quotes, floor plans, inspiration photos.
4. Start chats **inside** the project. Each one knows the instructions, files and (optionally) earlier project chats.

Great project ideas: a job hunt, a home renovation, a small business, a class you're taking, planning a wedding, your
health journal, a novel.

> [!NOTE]
> **📌 What happened to custom GPTs?**
> For years, "GPTs" let you build custom versions of ChatGPT. In September 2026 OpenAI announced it's **retiring custom
> GPTs** (they stop running on **December 11, 2026**) in favor of **plugins**, which combine reusable instructions
> (called **skills**) with connected apps. Personal accounts can no longer create new GPTs. If you rely on one, follow
> OpenAI's migration prompts, and use **Projects** for reusable instructions in the meantime.

## 🧠 Memory and personalization

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

ChatGPT can remember things about you, like that you're vegetarian, so you don't have to repeat yourself. You can see
and delete what it remembers.

</details>

ChatGPT personalizes in three ways:

1. **Custom instructions**: what *you* write about yourself.
2. **Saved memories**: facts ChatGPT decides to remember ("Alex is training for a 10k"). You'll see "Memory updated."
3. **Chat history reference**: it can draw on your past conversations for context.

**Manage it:** Settings → **Personalization → Memory**: view, delete individual memories, or turn memory off. Say
*"Remember that I'm allergic to shellfish"* or *"Forget that I live in Leeds."* Use a **temporary chat** (toggle at the
top of a new chat) when you don't want anything remembered.

## 🎙️ Voice and vision

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

You can talk to ChatGPT out loud, and show it things with your camera or screen while you talk.

</details>

- **Voice mode:** tap the **voice/waveform** icon. Talk naturally, interrupt any time, and choose a voice in settings.
- **Video and screen sharing** (mobile): during voice mode, turn on the **camera** (*"which of these cables goes into
  the router?"*) or **share your screen** (*"help me fill in this form"*).
- **Photos and files:** tap **+** to upload images, PDFs, spreadsheets and documents.
- **Dictation:** tap the microphone to speak a message instead of typing.

## 🖼️ Creating images

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Describe a picture and ChatGPT draws it. You can also upload a photo and ask it to change things.

</details>

Just ask in chat (*"Create an image of…"*) or use the **Images** tool. ChatGPT is especially good at:

- **Text inside images:** posters, invitations, labels, infographics that actually spell things right
- **Editing your photos:** *"Put this photo of my dog in a Renaissance painting,"* *"Remove the person in the
  background"*
- **Consistent characters and styles** across a series (a comic strip, a set of stickers)
- **Following detailed instructions:** layouts, colors, specific objects

**Recipe:** *"A cheerful poster for our street's summer party, Saturday 12 July, 2–6pm, Maple Close. Bunting,
ice cream, a bouncy castle. Bright, friendly, flat illustration style. Portrait format."* Then steer: *"Make the
date bigger,"* *"Add a dog."*

## 🌐 Search and deep research

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

ChatGPT can look things up on the internet and show you where it found them. For big questions, deep research reads lots
of websites and writes you a full report.

</details>

- **Search:** ChatGPT searches the web automatically when a question needs fresh info, or tap **Search** to force it.
  Answers show **source links**; click them to check.
- **Deep research:** choose **Deep research** from the tools menu (+) and ask a big question (*"Compare the best
  electric cars under $40k for a family of 5 in a cold climate"*). It asks clarifying questions, reads many sources for
  several minutes, and produces a structured, cited report.
- **Shopping research:** ask for product recommendations and ChatGPT can compare options, and in some countries you can
  buy from some merchants right in the chat.

## 🔌 Apps, plugins and connectors

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

You can plug other apps into ChatGPT, like Spotify or your Google Drive, so it can use them for you right in the chat.

</details>

ChatGPT connects to other services in a few ways:

- **Plugins** (called "apps" until mid-2026): mention one by name (*"Spotify, make a playlist for a rainy Sunday"*,
  *"Canva, turn this into a poster"*) and it appears inside the chat, sometimes with buttons and forms. Browse them in
  the plugin directory. Plugins can also bundle **skills** (reusable instructions), which is how they replace custom
  GPTs.
- **Connectors:** link Gmail, Google Drive, Outlook, SharePoint, GitHub, Dropbox and more so ChatGPT can search and use
  *your* content (*"Find the latest invoice from the plumber in my email"*).
- **Developer mode** (some plans): add any remote MCP server ([MCP Explained](../part-4-mcp-and-connectors/38-mcp-explained.md)).

Manage everything in **Settings** (look for **Plugins** and **Connectors**). Only connect what you need, and review permissions. More in
[Built-in Connectors](../part-4-mcp-and-connectors/41-built-in-connectors.md).

## 🤖 Agent mode and ChatGPT Work

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

In agent mode, ChatGPT can go on websites and do jobs for you, like researching hotels or filling in forms, while you
watch. Work mode can make whole documents, spreadsheets and slides.

</details>

This is where ChatGPT stops just talking and starts **doing**:

- **Agent mode** (paid plans): ChatGPT uses its own browser to navigate websites, click, fill forms, compare options and
  put together results. You watch it work, can take over at any time, and it asks permission before important actions
  like purchases. *"Find three family-friendly holiday cottages in Cornwall for the second week of August with a garden,
  under £1,200, and put them in a comparison table."*
- **ChatGPT Work** (desktop app, paid plans): give it a goal and it plans the steps (you approve the plan), gathers
  info across your connected apps, and produces **finished documents, spreadsheets, slides or sites**, working through
  longer projects with check-ins.
- **Scheduled tasks:** *"Every Monday at 8am, give me a summary of the week's weather and my calendar."* ChatGPT runs it
  and notifies you.

> [!WARNING]
> **⚠️ Supervise agents**
> Agents can make mistakes and can be tricked by malicious web pages. Watch what they do, don't hand over passwords
> unnecessarily, and approve purchases yourself. See [Computer Use & Browser
> Agents](../part-7-building-with-ai/71-computer-use-and-browser-agents.md).

## ✍️ Canvas, study mode and Health

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Canvas is a side page for editing writing together. Study mode turns ChatGPT into a tutor. Health helps you understand
your own health records.

</details>

- **Canvas:** for longer writing or code, ChatGPT opens a side-by-side editor. Highlight a paragraph and ask for changes,
  adjust length or reading level, and edit directly.
- **Study mode:** turns ChatGPT into a tutor that asks questions and gives hints instead of answers. Brilliant for
  students (and grown-ups learning anything). Choose it from the tools menu.
- **ChatGPT Health** (US, 18+): connect Apple Health and your medical records so ChatGPT can explain *your* lab results,
  medications and activity, in a separate, specially protected space. It's for understanding and preparing, not
  replacing your doctor.

## 💻 Codex: ChatGPT for coding

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Codex is ChatGPT's coding helper. It can write and fix whole programs for you, even from your phone.

</details>

**Codex** is OpenAI's coding agent, built into the desktop app (the **Codex** mode), the web, a command-line tool, and
code editors. It can build features, fix bugs and review code in your projects, running tasks in the background, and you
can even check on work from your phone. If you're curious about building apps, see [Agents & AI Coding
Tools](../part-7-building-with-ai/60-agents-and-coding-tools.md) and
[Vibe Coding Your First Real App](../part-7-building-with-ai/65-vibe-coding-your-first-app.md).

## 🍳 Step-by-step recipes

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Four real jobs you can do with ChatGPT today, click by click.

</details>

**Recipe 1: A personal meal-planning project**

1. New project → "🍽️ Family meals" → instructions: household, dislikes, budget, cooking time.
2. Chat: *"Plan next week's dinners with a shopping list by aisle."*
3. Next week, open the project and say *"Same again, but no repeats from last week."*

**Recipe 2: Turn a pile of documents into a decision**

1. Upload three quotes (PDFs) from builders.
2. *"Compare these quotes in a table: price, timeline, what's included and excluded, and red flags. Which questions
   should I ask each builder?"*

**Recipe 3: Practice a job interview by voice**

1. Voice mode → *"You're interviewing me for a [role]. Ask one question at a time, wait for my answer, and give me
   feedback after each."*
2. Afterwards, in text: *"Summarize my three weakest answers and how to improve them."*

**Recipe 4: A weekly briefing**

1. *"Every Friday at 5pm, give me three interesting things that happened this week in [renewable energy], with
   sources, in under 150 words."*
2. Adjust or cancel it later from the scheduled tasks list.

## 🔐 Privacy and settings

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

The important switches: whether your chats train the AI, what it remembers, and secret chats that aren't saved.

</details>

| Setting | Where | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| **Improve the model for everyone** | Settings → Data controls | Turn off if you'd rather your chats weren't used for training |
| **Memory** | Settings → Personalization | Review now and then; delete anything too personal |
| **Temporary chat** | Toggle at the top of a new chat | Use for sensitive questions (not saved to history or memory) |
| **Shared links** | Settings → Data controls → Shared links | Delete links you no longer want public |
| **Export / delete data** | Settings → Data controls | Download your data or delete your account |
| **Parental controls** | Settings → Parental controls | Link a teen's account, set quiet hours and limits |
| **Multi-factor auth** | Settings → Security | Turn it on |

## 💡 Pro tips and limitations

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Clever tricks power users know, and the things ChatGPT still isn't good at.

</details>

**Pro tips**

- ⌨️ **Keyboard shortcuts** in the desktop app speed everything up (look for the shortcut list in the help menu).
- 🔎 **Search your history:** the sidebar search finds old chats by content.
- 🧠 **"What do you know about me?"** reveals your memory profile. It's a good privacy check.
- 📎 **Mix modes:** upload a spreadsheet, then *"make a chart, then turn the findings into three slides."*
- 🎭 **Ask for a different perspective:** *"Answer as a skeptical accountant would."*

**Limitations**

- It can still **hallucinate**, especially without search. Check important facts
  ([chapter 10](../part-1-ai-from-zero/10-when-ai-gets-it-wrong.md)).
- **Usage limits** on free and lower tiers can interrupt long sessions.
- Features and names **change frequently**, and some launch in the US first.
- Agents are powerful but **slow and imperfect**. Supervise them.

## 🎯 Key takeaways

- ChatGPT is the **all-rounder**: chat, voice, vision, images, search, deep research, memory, apps and agents.
- Use **Auto** most of the time and **Thinking** for hard problems.
- **Projects** keep topics organized with shared instructions and files; **plugins and skills** are replacing custom
  GPTs.
- **Agent mode, ChatGPT Work and scheduled tasks** let it do multi-step jobs. Supervise them.
- Check **Data controls, Memory and temporary chats** for privacy.
