# 24 · Meta AI: The Complete Guide 👓

> ⏱️ 6 min read · 🎯 WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook users · 🧰 Needs: WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger or Facebook (or the Meta AI app)

**Meta AI is the assistant you probably already have: it's built into WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and Facebook,
used by billions of people.** No new app, no new account: just open a chat and ask. It also powers Ray-Ban Meta and
Oakley Meta smart glasses, creates images and videos, and has its own app. Because it's woven into social apps run by an
advertising company, knowing the privacy settings matters. This guide covers both.

<details class="eli5" open>
<summary>🧸 ELI5: This chapter in 30 seconds</summary>

Meta AI lives inside WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook. You can chat with it like a friend, ask it to settle a debate in
your group chat, make funny pictures, or ask your smart glasses "what am I looking at?" Just remember: Meta uses your AI
chats to personalize what you see (including ads), so don't tell it secrets.

</details>

## 🚪 Getting started

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

In WhatsApp or Instagram, look for the colorful ring icon or search for Meta AI, and start chatting. There's also a
Meta AI app.

</details>

- **WhatsApp:** tap the colorful **Meta AI** ring (on the Chats screen or in the search bar) and start chatting. In a
  group, type **@Meta AI** followed by your question.
- **Instagram / Messenger / Facebook:** search "Meta AI" or tap the Meta AI icon in messages or the search bar.
- **Meta AI app** (and **meta.ai** on the web): the full experience, with voice, image and video creation, memory and
  your glasses settings.
- **Glasses:** pair Ray-Ban Meta or Oakley Meta glasses through the **Meta AI** app and say *"Hey Meta…"*

> [!NOTE]
> **📌 What Meta AI can and can't see**
> Meta AI only sees messages you send **to it** (or messages in a group that **mention @Meta AI**, plus what you reply
> to). Your regular personal WhatsApp messages stay end-to-end encrypted and aren't read by Meta AI. But anything you
> *do* send to Meta AI can be used by Meta.

## 🎨 Imagine: images and video

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Type "Imagine" and describe a picture, and Meta AI makes it. It can also turn pictures into short videos.

</details>

- **Images:** type *"Imagine a cat astronaut floating past Saturn, cartoon style"* in any Meta AI chat.
- **Edit photos:** upload a picture and ask for changes (backgrounds, styles, adding things).
- **Animate:** turn an image into a short video clip.
- **Vibes:** a feed of short AI-generated videos in the Meta AI app, where you can create and remix your own.
- **Stickers and effects:** AI stickers and restyle effects appear throughout Meta's apps.

## 🕶️ Smart glasses: AI you wear

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Meta's smart glasses have a camera, speakers and Meta AI inside. You can ask "what am I looking at?" or get directions
without taking out your phone.

</details>

This is where Meta AI shines. With **Ray-Ban Meta**, **Oakley Meta** or the display-equipped models:

- 👀 *"Hey Meta, what am I looking at?"* (landmarks, plants, products, menus)
- 🌍 **Live translation** of conversations and signs
- 📸 Hands-free photos and videos (*"Hey Meta, take a video"*)
- 🎧 Music, calls and messages without your phone
- 🧭 Walking, cycling and transit directions on newer models
- ♿ Hugely helpful for **blind and low-vision** users (reading text, describing scenes, and connecting to volunteer
  helpers through Be My Eyes)

Some premium glasses features and higher usage limits come with the **Meta One** subscription.

## 🧠 Memory, voice and the Meta AI app

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

The Meta AI app lets you talk out loud with Meta AI, and it can remember things about you, like your favorite foods.

</details>

- **Voice:** natural, interruptible voice conversations in the app (and on glasses).
- **Memory:** Meta AI can remember details you share (*"I'm vegetarian," "I'm training for a marathon"*) and may use
  information from your Meta profiles. View and delete memories in the app's settings.
- **Discover feed:** see how others use Meta AI and share your own creations. **Be careful:** sharing to Discover makes
  things public, so double-check before you tap share.
- Meta is also rolling out more **agent-style** features (booking, tasks, connected apps) with its newer Muse models,
  starting in some countries and plans.

## 🔐 Privacy and settings

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Meta uses what you tell Meta AI to decide what posts and ads to show you. Keep private things out of it, and check what
you share publicly.

</details>

The key things to know:

- **Ads and content personalization:** Meta uses your interactions with Meta AI to personalize the content and ads you
  see across its apps (with regional exceptions, for example in parts of Europe). Treat AI chats accordingly.
- **Don't share sensitive information** (health, finances, other people's private details) with Meta AI.
- **Public by accident:** review anything before posting to the Discover feed; check your app's sharing settings.
- **Delete AI chats and memories:** in the Meta AI app settings, and in WhatsApp you can use commands or settings to
  reset Meta AI chats.
- **Privacy Center:** Meta's Privacy Center explains how AI uses your data and lets you object to certain uses where the
  law allows.

## 💡 Pro tips and limitations

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Clever tricks Meta AI fans know, and the things to watch out for.

</details>

**Pro tips**

- 👨‍👩‍👧 **Group-chat referee:** quick facts, recipe scaling, gift ideas, trivia nights.
- 🕶️ **Glasses + voice** is the most natural AI experience going: questions without screens.
- 🎨 **Stickers and restyles** make chats more fun.

**Limitations**

- Less powerful for **long documents, deep research or complex work** than ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude.
- **Privacy trade-offs** tied to Meta's advertising business.
- Features differ by **country** (especially in Europe).

## 🎯 Key takeaways

- Meta AI lives in **WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and Facebook**. No new app needed.
- Tag **@Meta AI** in group chats for shared answers; it only sees messages sent to it.
- **Imagine**, animation and **Vibes** make images and videos; **smart glasses** are its standout.
- Your AI chats can **personalize content and ads**, so keep sensitive info out, and check before sharing publicly.
