# 29 · Hubs & Specialty Chatbots 🧩

> ⏱️ 6 min read · 🎯 Explorers, privacy fans, learners and the curious · 🧰 Needs: nothing (most have free tiers)

**Beyond the big-name assistants there's a whole ecosystem of clever AI apps:** hubs that give you many models in one
place, privacy-first chatbots that don't keep your data, character and companion apps, AI tutors, and specialist tools
for writing, meetings and more. This chapter maps the landscape, points out the gems, and gives you a checklist for
deciding whether a new AI app deserves your data and your money.

<details class="eli5" open>
<summary>🧸 ELI5: This chapter in 30 seconds</summary>

There are lots of smaller AI apps too. Some let you use many different AIs in one place, some are extra private, some let
you chat with made-up characters, and some are special teachers for languages or school subjects. They can be great, but
check who made them and what they do with your information.

</details>

## 🧩 Multi-model hubs: many AIs, one app

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Hub apps let you talk to lots of different AIs (ChatGPT's brain, Claude's brain, Gemini's brain and more) in one place,
so you can compare them easily.

</details>

Hubs let you use models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, DeepSeek, Mistral and others under **one account**, often
one subscription. Great for comparing answers and trying new models the day they launch.

| Hub | What it's like | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| **Poe** (by Quora) | A polished app with dozens of models plus bots made by others; points-based subscription | Comparing models side by side, trying image/video models, building simple bots |
| **HuggingChat** (by Hugging Face) | Free chat with leading **open** models | Trying open models without installing anything |
| **OpenRouter** | One account and one bill for hundreds of models, with a simple chat page | Pay-as-you-go access; developers and tinkerers |
| **Perplexity Pro** | Choose GPT, Claude, Gemini, Sonar and others for answers with sources | Research with a model of your choice ([chapter 23](23-perplexity.md)) |
| **Microsoft/GitHub, Amazon Bedrock and cloud playgrounds** | Model playgrounds aimed at developers | Building apps ([Calling AI APIs Directly](../part-7-building-with-ai/67-calling-ai-apis.md)) |

**Trade-offs:** hubs may not have every feature of the original apps (voice modes, memory, connectors, agents), and your
data passes through both the hub and the model provider.

## 🔒 Privacy-first assistants

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Some AI apps are built to protect your privacy: they don't keep your chats or use them for training, and some even lock
your chats so the company can't read them.

</details>

| Assistant | Privacy approach |
|---|---|
| 🦆 **Duck.ai** (DuckDuckGo) | Free, no account; conversations are **anonymized** (the model providers don't see who you are) and not used for training |
| 🔐 **Proton Lumo** | From the makers of Proton Mail: **zero-access encryption** for chats, files and memories (Proton itself can't read them), no logs, European servers |
| 🦁 **Brave Leo** | Built into the Brave browser; designed not to retain your conversations |
| 🔍 **Kagi Assistant** | Part of the paid Kagi search engine; choose among top models without ads or tracking |
| 🏠 **Local AI** | The ultimate: run models on your own computer so nothing leaves it ([Part IX · Local AI](../part-9-local-ai/index.md)) |

These are perfect for **sensitive-but-everyday** questions (health worries, personal situations, money) where you'd
rather not build a profile anywhere. The trade-off: usually fewer bells and whistles.

> [!WARNING]
> **⚠️ "Uncensored" doesn't mean "safe"**
> Some apps advertise "no filters." Be cautious: they may lack safeguards against harmful content, and some have weak
> privacy despite their marketing. Check who runs them and read reviews.

## 🎭 Characters and companions

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Some apps let you chat with pretend characters or an AI "friend." They can be fun, but they're not real friends, and they
aren't right for kids.

</details>

| App | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Character.AI** | Chat with user-created characters (historical figures, fictional heroes, practice partners). **Since late 2025, open-ended chats are for 18+ only**; teens are limited to structured stories and creative features |
| **Replika** and similar | "AI companion" apps designed for ongoing personal conversation |
| **Grok companions** | Animated characters in the Grok app ([chapter 22](22-grok.md)) |
| **Pi** (Inflection AI) | A gentle, supportive conversational assistant focused on talking things through |

**Healthy use:** these apps can be fun, creative and even comforting. But they're designed to be engaging, sometimes
*very* engaging. Watch your time, keep investing in human relationships, never share secrets or money, and keep
children and teens away from open-ended companion bots. If you're struggling, please reach out to real people and
professional support. 💛

<details class="eli5">

Some AIs are made just for learning: they teach you step by step instead of just giving answers.

</details>

## ✅ Should you trust a new AI app? A checklist

<details class="eli5">
<summary>🧸 ELI5</summary>

Before you give a new AI app your information or money, check who made it, what it does with your data, and what other
people say about it.

</details>

Before signing up (and especially before paying or uploading personal data):

- [ ] **Who makes it?** A known company with a real address, or an anonymous app?
- [ ] **What model does it use?** Many apps are "wrappers" around ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Is the extra value worth it?
- [ ] **Privacy policy:** does it train on your data? Sell it? Where is it stored? Can you delete it?
- [ ] **Price:** watch for weekly subscriptions and hard-to-cancel trials.
- [ ] **Reviews:** what do independent reviewers and users say?
- [ ] **Lock-in:** can you export your data if you leave?
- [ ] **Age and safety:** appropriate for who's using it?

## 🎯 Key takeaways

- **Hubs** (Poe, HuggingChat, OpenRouter) give you many models in one place, which is great for comparing.
- **Privacy-first** assistants (Duck.ai, Proton Lumo, Brave Leo, local AI) suit sensitive questions.
- **Companion and character apps** can be fun but need boundaries, and aren't for kids.
- **Specialist tools** for learning, writing, meetings and creating often beat general assistants at one job.
- Use the **trust checklist** before handing any app your data or money.

## 🧠 Check yourself

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 1. You want to compare answers from several models without five subscriptions. What kind of tool?</summary>

A **multi-model hub**, like Poe, HuggingChat or OpenRouter.

</details>

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 2. Which assistant uses encryption so even the company can't read your chats?</summary>

**Proton Lumo** (zero-access encryption). Duck.ai anonymizes chats; local AI keeps everything on your device.

</details>

<details class="quiz">
<summary>❓ 3. Your 14-year-old wants to use Character.AI to chat with characters. What's the situation?</summary>

Since late 2025, **open-ended character chats are limited to 18+**. Teens get restricted story features. Talk with them
about companion apps generally.

</details>
